CUSAT's story is one of steady, purposeful growth rooted in Kerala's unique maritime and scientific heritage. What began as a modest School of Marine Sciences in colonial-era India has evolved, over more than eight decades, into one of the country's most respected autonomous universities. As you explore this history, you will see how each decade brought new disciplines, new campuses, and a deepening commitment to research and public service.

## Milestones Timeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="1938 — The Seed is Planted">
    The **School of Marine Sciences** is established, marking the first organised effort to bring scientific education to the Cochin region. This institution would become the nucleus around which the entire university later grew.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1958 — Marine Biology Takes Root">
    The **M.Sc. Marine Biology** programme is launched, one of the earliest postgraduate marine science programmes in India, reflecting the region's deep connection with the sea.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1962 — Legal Education Begins">
    The **School of Legal Studies** is established, broadening the institution's scope beyond the natural sciences and laying the foundation for a multi-disciplinary academic culture.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1964 — Management Education Pioneers">
    The **School of Management Studies** is founded, making it one of only **five premier management institutions** in India at the time — a remarkable distinction that underlines CUSAT's early commitment to professional and applied education.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1971 — University of Cochin Formed">
    The **University of Cochin** is officially constituted, bringing together the existing schools under a single university framework. The same year sees the founding of the **Department of Polymer Science and Rubber Technology**, capitalising on Kerala's natural rubber industry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1975 — Engineering and Library Infrastructure">
    The **Department of Electronics** and the **Department of Ship Technology** are established, followed by the inauguration of the **Central Library**, providing students with a growing repository of knowledge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1976 — A Wave of New Departments">
    Multiple new departments are added in a single year: **Industrial Fisheries**, **Applied Chemistry**, **Marine Geology**, and several others, rapidly expanding the university's academic portfolio and research capacity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1978 — Engineering Gets Its Own School">
    The **School of Engineering** is formally established, consolidating the engineering disciplines under one roof and setting the stage for decades of technical education and research.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1983 — Environmental Consciousness">
    The **School of Environmental Studies** is founded, placing CUSAT ahead of the curve in recognising the importance of environmental science at a time when the field was still emerging globally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1984 — Computer Science Arrives">
    The **Department of Computer Science** is established, ensuring that CUSAT keeps pace with the technological revolution that would reshape the world in the decades ahead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1986 — Reorganised as CUSAT">
    The university is formally reorganised and renamed **Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT)**, reflecting its sharpened focus on science, technology, and applied research. This reorganisation marks the beginning of the modern era for the institution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1990 — Computer Centre Inaugurated">
    The **Computer Centre** is inaugurated by the **Prime Minister of India**, a landmark event that signals CUSAT's growing national prominence and its commitment to computing infrastructure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1991 — Life Sciences Join the Family">
    The **Department of Biotechnology** is established, adding a critical new dimension to CUSAT's science portfolio just as India's biotechnology sector begins to take shape.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1995 — B.Tech Programmes and Photonics">
    CUSAT launches its **B.Tech programmes**, democratising access to undergraduate engineering education. The **International School of Photonics** is also established, making CUSAT one of the few Indian universities with dedicated photonics research at this time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1999 — Pulincunnu Campus Established">
    **CUCEK (Cochin University College of Engineering, Kuttanad)** is established at the Pulincunnu Campus in Alappuzha District, extending CUSAT's reach into the backwater region of Kerala and broadening access to quality engineering education.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2000 — Aquatic Animal Health">
    The **National Centre for Aquatic Animal Health** is established, reinforcing CUSAT's leadership in marine and aquatic sciences and supporting Kerala's vital fisheries sector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2010 — Women's Studies Centre">
    The **Women's Studies Centre** is founded, reflecting the university's commitment to gender equity, social justice, and interdisciplinary social science research.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2012 — Budget Studies Centre">
    The **Centre for Budget Studies** is established, creating a dedicated space for research and analysis of public finance — an increasingly important area of policy-relevant inquiry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A Legacy of Firsts

Throughout its history, CUSAT has distinguished itself by being ahead of the curve — whether launching one of India's first management schools, establishing a department of environmental studies before it became fashionable, or creating a dedicated photonics institute. This spirit of anticipating the future continues to define the university today.

<Note>
  CUSAT's evolution from a single School of Marine Sciences in 1938 to a multi-campus, multi-disciplinary university with 30+ departments is a testament to the vision of its founders and the dedication of generations of faculty, students, and administrators.
</Note>

## Continuity and Change

The federal university model that CUSAT adopted has allowed each school and department to develop its own identity and specialisation, while remaining part of a larger academic community. As you engage with CUSAT — whether as a student, researcher, or partner — you are joining an institution whose history of bold academic choices continues to shape its present and future.
