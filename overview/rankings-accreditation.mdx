## NAAC A+ Accreditation

The **National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC)** is the apex body in India responsible for assessing the quality of higher education institutions. Achieving an **A+ Grade** in the 3rd cycle of re-accreditation is a significant distinction — it signals that CUSAT consistently meets the highest benchmarks for:

- **Curricular aspects** — programme design, flexibility, and relevance
- **Teaching-learning and evaluation** — faculty quality, student support, and assessment rigour
- **Research, innovations, and extension** — publications, patents, and community outreach
- **Infrastructure and learning resources** — libraries, labs, and digital facilities
- **Student support and progression** — scholarships, placements, and welfare services
- **Governance, leadership, and management** — transparency and strategic planning
- **Institutional values and best practices** — ethics, sustainability, and inclusivity

<Info>
  NAAC's A+ Grade is awarded only to institutions that demonstrate exceptional performance across all seven criteria. CUSAT's 3rd cycle re-accreditation with this grade confirms a sustained, long-term commitment to quality rather than a one-time achievement.
</Info>

## NBA Reaccreditation — School of Engineering

All B.Tech programmes offered at CUSAT's **School of Engineering** have been reaccredited by the **National Board of Accreditation (NBA)** under the **Tier I category** — the highest tier for engineering programmes. This accreditation is valid until **December 2028**, providing you with the confidence that your engineering degree meets stringent outcome-based education standards aligned with global norms including the Washington Accord.

<Note>
  NBA Tier I accreditation means that your B.Tech degree from CUSAT's School of Engineering is recognised by signatory countries of the Washington Accord, significantly enhancing your eligibility for professional engineering roles and further studies abroad.
</Note>

## International Rankings

### Times Higher Education (THE)

CUSAT's continued presence in the **Times Higher Education World University Rankings** since 2017 reflects its performance across five pillars: teaching, research, citations (research influence), industry income, and international outlook. Appearing in the THE rankings places CUSAT in the company of the world's leading research universities.

### QS World University Rankings

The **QS Rankings** evaluate universities on academic reputation, employer reputation, faculty-student ratio, citations per faculty, international faculty ratio, and international student ratio. CUSAT's listing in the QS rankings confirms that it is recognised not only within India but also by the global academic and employer community.

## Innovation Recognition — ARIIA 2021

The **Atal Ranking of Institutions on Innovation Achievements (ARIIA)** is a Government of India initiative that ranks higher education institutions on their innovation and entrepreneurship ecosystem. CUSAT's recognition under **ARIIA 2021** acknowledges the work of its Atal Incubation Centre, technology transfer activities, and the culture of start-up thinking cultivated across its campuses.

<Tip>
  If you are an aspiring entrepreneur or innovator, CUSAT's ARIIA-recognised ecosystem offers you mentorship, incubation support, and funding guidance to turn your ideas into ventures.
</Tip>

## Quality Assurance Infrastructure

CUSAT's **Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)** is the institutional body responsible for planning, guiding, and monitoring quality enhancement activities. The IQAC:

- Prepares the **Annual Quality Assurance Report (AQAR)**
- Facilitates **academic and administrative audits**
- Coordinates **stakeholder feedback** from students, alumni, and employers
- Drives **curriculum revision** and programme outcome assessment cycles

<Info>
  All IQAC activities, reports, and quality documentation are publicly accessible at **[qc.cusat.ac.in](https://qc.cusat.ac.in)**. You can review CUSAT's quality journey in full transparency at any time.
</Info>
