Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT) stands as one of India's foremost autonomous universities, nestled in the vibrant city of Kochi, Kerala. Founded in 1971 as the University of Cochin and reorganised under its current name in 1986, CUSAT has grown into a nationally and globally recognised institution that blends cutting-edge research with rigorous academic training. Whether you are a prospective student, a researcher, or an industry partner, CUSAT offers a dynamic environment where discovery and learning go hand in hand.

## Academic Breadth

CUSAT operates on a **federal university model**, giving its constituent schools and departments significant academic autonomy while maintaining a unified vision. You will find programmes across science, technology, marine sciences, law, management, social sciences, and the arts — making CUSAT a truly multi-disciplinary institution.

<Info>
  CUSAT offers **24 undergraduate**, **55 postgraduate**, **9 pre-doctoral**, and **28 doctoral programmes**, ensuring pathways for learners at every stage of their academic journey.
</Info>

The university's three campuses — the sprawling 180-acre Main Campus at Thrikkakara, the Lakeside Marine Sciences Campus at Pallimukku, and the Pulincunnu Campus in Kuttanad — each provide specialised environments tailored to their respective disciplines. Across these campuses, you gain access to world-class laboratories, libraries, and research facilities.

## Research and Innovation

Research is central to life at CUSAT. The university's faculty and students regularly publish in leading international journals, collaborate with industry partners, and contribute to policy-making bodies. CUSAT's Atal Incubation Centre and technology transfer office support entrepreneurship and start-up culture, ensuring that innovation created on campus reaches the wider world.

<Tip>
  If you are interested in research opportunities, explore the individual department pages to find faculty working on topics that align with your interests. CUSAT's research ecosystem spans marine biology, photonics, polymer science, atmospheric sciences, and many more frontier areas.
</Tip>

## International Collaborations

CUSAT maintains active partnerships with reputed universities and research institutions across the globe. These collaborations enable student and faculty exchange programmes, joint research projects, and co-supervision of doctoral candidates. As a student, you benefit from an internationally connected environment that broadens your perspective and enhances your employability on a global scale.
