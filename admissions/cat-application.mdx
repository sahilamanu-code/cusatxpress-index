Applying for the CUSAT Common Admission Test is done entirely online through the university's official admissions portal at **admissions.cusat.ac.in**. The process is straightforward — you create an account, select your programme(s), fill in your details, upload supporting documents, and pay the application fee. Once your application is successfully submitted, you will receive a registration number that you use for all future correspondence, including hall ticket download and allotment participation. Read through this guide carefully before you begin so you have everything ready.

<Tip>
  Start your application well before the deadline. Seats are limited and the counselling process is rank-based, so applying early gives you maximum time to double-check your documents, correct any errors before the portal closes, and avoid last-minute fee payment failures.
</Tip>

## What You Need Before You Start

Gather the following before opening the application portal:

**Documents to Upload (scanned copies / digital images):**
- Recent passport-size photograph (white background, taken within the last 3 months)
- Scanned signature on white paper
- Class 10 mark sheet and certificate (for date of birth proof)
- Class 12 / qualifying examination mark sheet(s)
- Transfer Certificate (TC) / Migration Certificate from your last institution
- Category certificate (SC / ST / OBC / EWS) — if applicable, issued by a competent authority
- Any other certificates specified in the prospectus for your programme (e.g., sports, PwD)

**Technical requirements:**
- Photograph: JPG/JPEG, typically 10–100 KB, specified dimensions (check portal instructions)
- Signature: JPG/JPEG, typically 5–50 KB
- Mark sheets / certificates: PDF or JPG, as specified on the portal

**Other essentials:**
- A valid, regularly checked email address (all communications will be sent here)
- A working mobile number (OTPs and SMS alerts are sent to this number)
- A debit card, credit card, or net banking account for fee payment

## Step-by-Step Application Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account on the Admissions Portal">
    Go to [admissions.cusat.ac.in](https://admissions.cusat.ac.in) and click **New Registration** or **Apply Now**. Enter your full name (as it appears on your Class 10 certificate), a valid email address, and your mobile number. You will receive a one-time password (OTP) to verify both. Once verified, set a secure password for your account. Keep your login credentials safe — you will need them throughout the admissions cycle, including for hall ticket download and allotment participation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Programme(s) and Verify Eligibility">
    After logging in, browse the list of available programmes and select the one(s) you wish to apply for. Before proceeding, carefully read the eligibility criteria displayed for each programme. Confirm that your qualifying examination, subjects studied, and percentage of marks meet the minimum requirements. If you are applying to more than one programme, you may need to submit separate applications and fees for each. Note the paper code for the CAT paper you are required to appear for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill In Your Application Form">
    Complete all sections of the online application form:
    - **Personal details:** Name, date of birth, gender, category, nationality, contact details, and address
    - **Academic qualifications:** Board/university name, year of passing, subjects studied, marks obtained, and percentage for each qualifying exam
    - **Programme preferences:** The specific programme and, where applicable, campus or department preference
    - **Reservation details:** If you belong to a reserved category, indicate this and be ready to upload the relevant certificate

    Review every field before moving to the next section. Errors in your name or date of birth can cause problems at the exam centre and during document verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Required Documents">
    Upload each document in the format and size specified on the portal. Typical requirements:

    | Document | Format | Approximate Size |
    |---|---|---|
    | Passport photograph | JPG/JPEG | 10–100 KB |
    | Signature | JPG/JPEG | 5–50 KB |
    | Class 10 mark sheet | PDF / JPG | Up to 500 KB |
    | Class 12 / qualifying mark sheet | PDF / JPG | Up to 500 KB |
    | Category certificate (if applicable) | PDF / JPG | Up to 500 KB |

    Ensure scans are clear, fully legible, and not cropped. Blurry or incomplete uploads are a common reason for application rejection or issues at document verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the Application Fee">
    After completing the form and uploading documents, you will be directed to the fee payment page. The application fee is paid **online** through the portal's integrated payment gateway (SBI Collect or another authorised gateway).

    | Category | Approximate Fee |
    |---|---|
    | General / OBC | ₹1,000 per application |
    | SC / ST | ₹500 per application |

    <Note>
      The exact fee amount is published in the official prospectus each year and may differ from the figures above. Confirm the current fee on the admissions portal before making payment. The fee is **non-refundable** once paid, even if you later decide not to appear for the exam.
    </Note>

    After successful payment, an online receipt or transaction ID is generated. Save this receipt — you may need it if there is any discrepancy in your payment status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Your Application Confirmation">
    Once payment is confirmed, your application is submitted. Download and save the **application confirmation / acknowledgement page**, which contains your unique **registration number**. Print a copy and keep it safely. This number is required for all future correspondence with the Directorate of Admissions and for tracking your application status on the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Your Hall Ticket">
    Approximately one to two weeks before the exam, log back into the admissions portal and download your **hall ticket (admit card)**. The hall ticket contains:
    - Your name, registration number, and photograph
    - Your assigned **test centre** name and address
    - Your **exam date and reporting time**
    - General instructions for the exam day

    Print the hall ticket on A4 paper. Carry it to the exam centre along with a valid photo identity document (Aadhaar card, passport, or any government-issued ID). Entry to the exam hall is not permitted without the hall ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Appear for the CUSAT CAT">
    Report to your assigned test centre at least **30 minutes before** your scheduled exam time. Carry your printed hall ticket and a valid photo ID. The exam is computer-based — you will be seated at a designated computer terminal. Calculators, mobile phones, smartwatches, and electronic devices are not permitted inside the exam hall.

    After completing the exam, your responses are automatically submitted. Do not close the browser or shut down the system until the submission confirmation appears on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Rank List and Participate in Allotment">
    Results and the programme-wise rank list are published on the admissions portal in May. Log in to check your rank. If you qualify, you will be eligible to participate in the **centralised allotment (counselling) rounds**:

    1. Submit your programme and campus preferences online during the preference entry window.
    2. Seat allotment is made based on your rank, preferences, category, and seat availability.
    3. Accept your allotted seat within the specified deadline, pay the semester fee online, and upload the required documents.
    4. Report to your department on the joining date with all original documents for verification.

    If seats remain after all allotment rounds, you may also participate in **real-time / spot admissions** in August–September.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment Details

All fee payments — application fee, allotment acceptance fee, and semester tuition fees — are collected online. The portal typically integrates **SBI Collect** or another authorised payment gateway supporting:

- Net banking (all major banks)
- Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay)
- Credit cards
- UPI (where enabled)

Always verify that the transaction is marked **successful** before closing the browser. If money is debited but the portal does not update, wait 24–48 hours for reconciliation and then contact the Directorate of Admissions with your transaction ID.

## After Allotment: Document Verification Checklist

When you report to join your allotted programme, carry the following **original documents** along with one set of self-attested photocopies:

<Accordion title="View the document verification checklist">
  - Allotment order / acceptance printout from the portal
  - CUSAT CAT hall ticket
  - CUSAT CAT rank card / result printout
  - Class 10 mark sheet and passing certificate (original)
  - Class 12 / qualifying examination mark sheet(s) (original)
  - Transfer Certificate (TC) and Migration Certificate from your last institution
  - Conduct / Character Certificate
  - Category / caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) issued by a competent authority — if applicable
  - Income certificate — if applicable for fee concession
  - PwD certificate — if applicable
  - Passport-size photographs (6–10 copies)
  - Valid photo ID (Aadhaar, passport, or equivalent)
  - Fee payment receipts (application fee and allotment fee)
</Accordion>
