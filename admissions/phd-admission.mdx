CUSAT offers **28 doctoral programmes** spanning every major discipline taught at the university — from engineering and marine sciences to law, humanities, and management. PhD admissions are conducted through the **Doctoral Admission Test (DAT)**, a department-specific written test and interview process held annually. Candidates who hold a valid UGC-NET, CSIR-NET, or GATE qualification may be exempted from the written component of the DAT but are still required to appear for the interview. The university's Research and Development Cell (RDC) oversees research culture, infrastructure, and doctoral regulations.

## Eligibility

To be considered for PhD admission at CUSAT, you must hold a **Master's degree in a relevant subject** from a recognised university. The minimum marks requirement is:

- **55% aggregate** (or equivalent grade) for General and OBC candidates
- **50% aggregate** (or equivalent grade) for SC/ST candidates

Some departments may specify additional eligibility criteria based on the research area. Always verify the programme-specific requirements in the current DAT notification published on the admissions portal.

<Info>
  Candidates who have cleared the **UGC-JRF** examination are given additional priority and may be considered for institutional fellowships. Check with your department for fellowship availability.
</Info>

## The Doctoral Admission Test (DAT)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Application">
    Apply online through [admissions.cusat.ac.in](https://admissions.cusat.ac.in) when the DAT notification is published. Each department sets its own application window, so monitor the portal regularly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Written Test (DAT)">
    Eligible candidates appear for a department-specific written test that assesses subject knowledge, research aptitude, and analytical skills. Test dates vary by department.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interview">
    Shortlisted candidates from the written test (and NET/GATE-exempted candidates) appear before a departmental interview panel. The interview evaluates your research interest, proposed area of work, and academic background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rank List Publication">
    Two separate rank lists are published for each department:
    - **Group I** — Candidates with NET/GATE qualification
    - **Group II** — Candidates admitted through DAT score alone
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allotment & Enrolment">
    Candidates are allotted a supervisor and research topic based on availability and rank. Complete the admission formalities at your department and register with the Research and Development Cell (RDC).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## NET/GATE Exemption

If you hold a valid **UGC-NET, CSIR-NET, or GATE** score, you are **exempted from the written component** of the DAT. However, you must still:

- Apply within the advertised window
- Appear for the departmental **interview**
- Be ranked in **Group I** rank list

<Note>
  NET/GATE exemption applies only to the written test. Attendance at the interview is mandatory for all candidates, including those with qualifying scores.
</Note>
