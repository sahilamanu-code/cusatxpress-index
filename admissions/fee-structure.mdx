CUSAT publishes a comprehensive fee structure for all its programmes — undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral, and diploma — on the official portal at [cusat.ac.in/fee](https://cusat.ac.in/fee). Fees differ by programme, department, and category (General, OBC, SC/ST), and include components such as tuition fee, university fee, laboratory charges, and other institutional levies. In addition to the full fee schedule, CUSAT provides convenient **online payment options** through the State Bank of India's SBI Collect platform, allowing you to pay semester fees, examination fees, hostel rent, and other dues from anywhere.

<Note>
  Fee amounts are revised periodically by the university. Always verify the current fee applicable to your programme at [cusat.ac.in/fee](https://cusat.ac.in/fee) before making any payment. Do not rely on figures from previous years or unofficial sources.
</Note>

## Full Fee Schedule

The official fee schedule covers all schools, departments, and programme levels. You can view and download it at:

CUSAT has integrated with **SBI Collect**, the State Bank of India's secure online payment platform, for most fee categories. To pay, you will need the correct **Corporate ID (corpID)** for your school or category of payment. Using the wrong corpID may lead to incorrect allocation of your payment.

### How to Pay via SBI Collect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to SBI Collect">
    Visit the SBI Collect portal at [onlinesbi.sbi/sbicollect](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/sbicollect) or access it through the payment link on the CUSAT homepage at [cusat.ac.in](https://cusat.ac.in).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Educational Institution">
    Choose **Educational Institutions** as the category, then search for Cochin University of Science and Technology or enter the relevant corpID directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Correct corpID">
    Input the corpID applicable to your school or fee category (see table above). Verify that the institution name displayed matches your department before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Payment Details">
    Enter your registration number, semester, name, and the fee amount as indicated in your fee statement or challan. Double-check all details before confirming.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Payment">
    Pay using Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, or UPI. Save or print the **SBI Collect Reference Number (DU number)** as proof of payment — you will need this for admission formalities and records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Always keep a copy of your SBI Collect payment receipt. Disputes regarding fee payment can only be resolved with the transaction reference number. Payments made to incorrect corpIDs may require manual rectification, which can cause delays.
</Warning>

## CUSAT Online Payment Portal

In addition to SBI Collect, CUSAT provides a consolidated **Online Payment** link on its main website footer at [cusat.ac.in](https://cusat.ac.in). This portal may be used for:

- Application fees
- Miscellaneous departmental fees
- Certificate and document charges

Navigate to [cusat.ac.in](https://cusat.ac.in), scroll to the footer, and click **Online Payment** to access this facility.

| Category | Approximate Application Fee |

<Info>

## Fee Concessions and Scholarships

CUSAT students may be eligible for various scholarships and fee concessions offered by the Government of Kerala, Government of India, and the university:

- **Post-Matric Scholarship** — for SC/ST students via the Social Welfare Department, Kerala
- **OBC Scholarship** — Central government scholarship for OBC students
- **Merit-cum-Means Scholarship** — for economically weaker sections
- **GATE Scholarship** — for M.Tech students with valid GATE scores (₹12,400/month, as per AICTE norms)
- **University Merit Scholarships** — awarded to academic toppers in select programmes

Contact the Scholarship Section of the Students Affairs office for application procedures and deadlines.
