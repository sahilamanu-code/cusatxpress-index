CUSAT welcomes students from across the world through a dedicated international admissions pathway managed by the **Office of International Relations (OIR)**. Foreign nationals and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) who wish to study at CUSAT — whether at the undergraduate, postgraduate, or doctoral level — are not required to appear in the CUSAT Common Admission Test (CAT) in most cases. Instead, you apply directly through the OIR, which evaluates your academic credentials and guides you through the admission, visa, and onboarding process. CUSAT's growing network of international partnerships and its location in Kochi, one of India's most cosmopolitan cities, makes it an attractive destination for global learners.

## Who Can Apply

You are eligible to apply as an international student at CUSAT if you fall into one of the following categories:

- **Foreign Nationals** — Citizens of countries other than India holding a valid passport
- **Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs)** — Individuals holding a PIO card or OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) card
- **Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)** — Indian citizens residing abroad

<Info>
  In most cases, international applicants are **not required to appear in CUSAT CAT**. Admission is based on your academic transcripts and equivalence of qualifications. The OIR will advise you on any additional tests or documentation required for your specific programme.
</Info>

## Application Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Programme Availability">
    Visit [oir.cusat.ac.in](https://oir.cusat.ac.in) to confirm that your chosen programme is available for international students in the current academic year and to review the intake numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Your Documents">
    Gather certified copies of your academic transcripts, degree certificates, passport, visa details, English proficiency proof (if applicable), and any other documents specified by the OIR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Your Application">
    Complete the application form available on the OIR website and submit it along with required documents and the prescribed application fee to the OIR office.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credential Verification">
    The OIR verifies your academic credentials for equivalence with Indian qualifications. For doctoral admissions, a research proposal may also be required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Admission Offer & Visa">
    On successful verification, you receive an admission offer letter. Use this to apply for a **Student Visa (S-Visa)** through the Indian Embassy or Consulate in your country.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrive and Enrol">
    Report to CUSAT's OIR office upon arrival. The OIR team will assist you with registration, hostel allocation, and orientation. You will also be introduced to student support services on campus.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Study in India Programme

CUSAT participates in the **Study in India** initiative, a Government of India programme that makes Indian higher education accessible and attractive for international students through scholarships and simplified admission pathways.

- Programme portal: [studyinindia.gov.in](https://studyinindia.gov.in)
- Scholarships and fee waivers may be available for eligible international students under this scheme
- CUSAT-listed programmes on the portal carry the Study in India badge

<Info>
  The Study in India programme covers a wide range of CUSAT programmes. Visit [studyinindia.gov.in](https://studyinindia.gov.in) and search for "Cochin University of Science and Technology" to see eligible programmes and available scholarship amounts.
</Info>

## International Student Support Services

Once you arrive at CUSAT, the OIR continues to support you throughout your academic journey:

- **Orientation Programme** — Dedicated orientation for international students at the start of each academic year
- **Buddy Programme** — Pairing with CUSAT student volunteers to ease your transition
- **Visa and Documentation Assistance** — Help with visa extensions, FRRO registration, and related formalities
- **Cultural Integration** — Events and activities that connect international students with local culture and the wider university community
- **Academic Support** — Liaison with departments for any programme-related queries
