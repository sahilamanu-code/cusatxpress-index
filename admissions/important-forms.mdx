Navigating the paperwork for university admission and academic certification can be daunting. CUSAT maintains a centralised set of forms, regulations, brochures, and academic bulletins to make this process straightforward. Whether you are applying for admission, seeking an equivalence certificate for your foreign qualification, or looking for the curriculum structure of your programme, the resources below will point you to the right document. Most documents are available as downloadable PDFs from the CUSAT website, while all admission applications are submitted exclusively online.

<Note>
  Some forms and procedural documents are also available in physical copy from the **Directorate of Admissions** office at CUSAT. If you are unable to access a document online, visit or contact the office directly.
</Note>

## Admissions

All applications to CUSAT programmes — UG, PG, doctoral, and diploma — are submitted **online only** through the official admissions portal. No offline or paper-based applications are accepted.

<Card title="CUSAT Admissions Portal" icon="arrow-right-to-bracket">
  Register, fill your application form, upload documents, and track your application status entirely online.

  **[admissions.cusat.ac.in](https://admissions.cusat.ac.in)**
</Card>

<Tip>
  Keep scanned copies (PDF/JPG) of all your certificates, mark sheets, category certificate, and a passport photograph ready before you start the online application. This will help you complete the form in one sitting without interruptions.
</Tip>

## Certificate Forms

### Equivalence Certificate

If your qualifying degree was awarded by a university outside India, or if you have studied under a non-standard board, you may need an **Equivalence Certificate** from CUSAT to confirm that your qualification is equivalent to the corresponding Indian degree. This certificate is typically required for:

- Admission to higher programmes at CUSAT or other institutions
- Regulatory and accreditation purposes

The procedure document and the application form are available at:

**[cusat.ac.in](https://cusat.ac.in)** → *Uploads section* → **Equivalence Certificate**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the Procedure PDF">
    Visit the CUSAT website's uploads section and download the Equivalence Certificate procedure document. Read it carefully to understand the required documents and fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the Application Form">
    Complete the form (also available in the uploads section) with accurate details of your qualification, institution, and the purpose for which equivalence is sought.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit to the Examination Section">
    Submit the completed form along with certified copies of your certificates and the prescribed fee to the University Examination Section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Recognition Certificate

A **Recognition Certificate** is issued by CUSAT to confirm that a degree or qualification earned from another institution is recognised by the university. This is commonly required for:

- Employment verification
- Higher education applications at other institutions
- Government and public sector recruitment processes

The procedure PDF and application form are available at:

**[cusat.ac.in](https://cusat.ac.in)** → *Uploads section* → **Recognition Certificate**

<Info>
  The Equivalence Certificate and Recognition Certificate are distinct documents serving different purposes. If you are unsure which one you need, contact the Examination Section or the Directorate of Admissions for clarification.
</Info>

## Old / Historical Regulations

If you were admitted to CUSAT under an older academic scheme and need to refer to the regulations that governed your programme, historical regulation documents are archived at:

**[rekha.cusat.ac.in](https://rekha.cusat.ac.in)**

This archive is particularly useful for alumni seeking transcripts, degree verification, or re-admission under an old scheme.

<Warning>
  Current students should always refer to the latest regulations at [cusat.ac.in/student/regulations](https://cusat.ac.in/student/regulations). Old regulations at rekha.cusat.ac.in apply only to those admitted under the corresponding historical schemes and should not be used as a reference for ongoing programmes.
</Warning>
