The CUSAT Common Admission Test — commonly called **CUSAT CAT** — is Cochin University of Science and Technology's own annual entrance examination for admission to its undergraduate, postgraduate, and integrated programmes. It is entirely separate from the national-level CAT conducted by the IIMs for MBA admissions; CUSAT CAT is designed, conducted, and evaluated by the university itself. Every year, thousands of candidates from across India appear for this computer-based test to secure a seat in one of CUSAT's highly regarded programmes spanning engineering, science, law, management, and more.

## What Is the CUSAT CAT?

CUSAT CAT is a **computer-based test (CBT)** held once a year, typically in April. The question paper is tailored to the programme you apply for — engineering aspirants answer questions in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, while science and management applicants face papers relevant to their chosen discipline. The test is designed to assess subject knowledge, analytical ability, and problem-solving skills at a level appropriate for the programme's entry point.

<Note>
  CUSAT CAT is **not** the same as the national MBA-CAT (Common Admission Test) conducted by the Indian Institutes of Management. If you are applying to CUSAT's MBA programme, you must appear for CUSAT's own CAT unless otherwise specified in that year's prospectus.
</Note>

## Programmes Covered

CUSAT CAT serves as the gateway to a wide range of programmes offered by the university's schools and departments. Admission to all of the following programmes is based on CAT performance:

- **B.Tech** — Bachelor of Technology (Engineering, 4 years)
- **Integrated M.Sc.** — Five-year integrated science programmes
- **BBA LLB / B.Com LLB** — Integrated law programmes (School of Legal Studies)
- **M.Tech** — Master of Technology (Postgraduate Engineering)
- **M.Sc.** — Master of Science across various disciplines
- **MBA** — Master of Business Administration
- **LLM** — Master of Laws
- **PhD** — Doctoral programmes (via a separate Doctoral Admission Test in some departments)

## The Admissions Pipeline

The journey from aspiring applicant to enrolled student follows a clear, structured process. Understanding each stage helps you plan your preparation and never miss a critical deadline.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register and Apply">
    Create an account on the official admissions portal at **admissions.cusat.ac.in**, select your programme(s), fill in your details, upload documents, and pay the application fee online.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Hall Ticket">
    Once your application is verified, download your hall ticket from the portal one to two weeks before the exam. The hall ticket contains your test centre, date, and reporting time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Appear for the CAT">
    Sit the computer-based CUSAT CAT at your assigned test centre in April. The exam is conducted in multiple sessions depending on the number of applicants and programmes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rank List Publication">
    After the exam, CUSAT publishes a programme-wise rank list on the admissions portal. Your rank determines your eligibility for each allotment round.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Centralised Allotment (Counselling)">
    Participate in the centralised allotment process — typically two to three rounds held between May and July. You indicate your programme and campus preferences online, and seats are allotted based on rank, category, and availability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report and Join">
    Accept your allotted seat, pay the semester fee online, and report to your department on the specified joining date. Classes for the odd semester generally begin in July.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Real-Time / Spot Admissions">
    If seats remain vacant after the main allotment rounds, CUSAT conducts real-time or spot admissions, usually in August–September. Eligible candidates on the rank list can attend in person to claim remaining seats.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Computer-Based Test Format

The CAT is conducted entirely on computer at designated test centres. Key characteristics include:

- **Mode:** Online / Computer-Based Test (CBT)
- **Duration:** Typically 3 hours (programme-specific; refer to the current prospectus)
- **Question Type:** Multiple-choice questions (MCQs)
- **Subjects:** Determined by the programme applied for (e.g., Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics for B.Tech; subject-specific papers for M.Sc. and M.Tech)
- **Medium:** English
- **Negative Marking:** As specified in the official prospectus each year

<Info>
  Candidates applying to multiple programmes may need to appear for more than one paper. Check the official prospectus at **admissions.cusat.ac.in** to confirm the paper code and syllabus for each programme you intend to apply for.
</Info>

## Reserved Category Admissions

CUSAT follows the reservation policy mandated by the Government of Kerala and the University Act. SC, ST, OBC, and other reserved category candidates benefit from relaxed eligibility criteria, lower application fees, and seats reserved in every programme. Special admissions for SC/ST candidates are also conducted in accordance with statutory requirements.

## International Students

If you are an international student or a Non-Resident Indian (NRI), the admissions process is handled separately through the **Office of International Relations (OIR)**. Visit [oir.cusat.ac.in](https://oir.cusat.ac.in) for programme-specific requirements, fee structures, and application procedures.
