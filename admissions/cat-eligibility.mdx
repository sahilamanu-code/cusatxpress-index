Before you register for the CUSAT Common Admission Test, you must confirm that you meet the minimum educational qualifications for the programme you wish to join. Eligibility requirements vary by programme level and discipline — some programmes additionally specify which subjects you must have studied at the qualifying level. The criteria below reflect the general standards applied by CUSAT; always cross-check against the **official prospectus published each year** at admissions.cusat.ac.in, as minor adjustments can occur from cycle to cycle.

<Note>
  Specific eligibility criteria for each programme — including subject combinations, minimum marks, and age limits — are published in the official CUSAT prospectus every year at **admissions.cusat.ac.in**. The details below are general guidelines and must be verified against the current year's prospectus before you apply.
</Note>

## Undergraduate Programmes

### B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology)

The B.Tech programme is CUSAT's flagship four-year undergraduate engineering degree, admitting students directly after Class 12.

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Qualifying Exam | 10+2 or equivalent from a recognised board |
| Mandatory Subjects | Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) |
| Minimum Aggregate (PCM) | 50% for General / OBC candidates |
| Minimum Aggregate (SC/ST) | 45% in PCM |
| Age (Lower Limit) | 17 years as on 31 December of the admission year |
| Age (Upper Limit) | 25 years (relaxation applicable for reserved categories) |

<Info>
  Candidates appearing for their Class 12 board examinations in the same year may apply provisionally, subject to meeting the eligibility criteria once results are declared. Your admission will be cancelled if final results do not meet the minimum requirement.
</Info>

### BBA LLB / B.Com LLB (Integrated Law Programmes)

These five-year integrated programmes are offered by the School of Legal Studies. Eligibility is governed by Bar Council of India regulations and CUSAT's own School of Legal Studies rules. Candidates must have passed 10+2 or an equivalent examination from a recognised board with a minimum aggregate as specified in the School of Legal Studies regulations for the current year.

## Integrated Programmes

### Integrated M.Sc. (Five-Year)

The Integrated M.Sc. programme allows students to enrol directly after Class 12 and earn a postgraduate science degree in five years.

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Qualifying Exam | 10+2 or equivalent with Science subjects |
| Minimum Aggregate | 55% in the qualifying examination |
| Subject Requirements | Relevant science subjects depending on the specialisation (e.g., Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics or Biology) |

## Postgraduate Programmes

### M.Tech (Master of Technology)

M.Tech admissions at CUSAT recognise both GATE-qualified candidates and those who appear for CUSAT's own postgraduate CAT.

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Qualifying Degree | B.Tech / B.E. or equivalent in a relevant engineering or technology discipline |
| Preferred Qualification | Valid GATE score in the relevant paper |
| Without GATE | Candidates without a GATE score must appear for and qualify in the CUSAT CAT for M.Tech |
| Minimum Marks | As specified per department in the prospectus |

<Accordion title="Which disciplines are considered 'relevant' for M.Tech?">
  Relevance of your undergraduate discipline to the M.Tech specialisation is determined department by department. For example, a Computer Science & Engineering graduate can apply to M.Tech CSE or related IT programmes, while a Civil Engineering graduate applies to M.Tech Structural Engineering. Refer to the programme-specific eligibility table in the prospectus for the full list of qualifying disciplines.
</Accordion>

### M.Sc. (Master of Science)

CUSAT offers M.Sc. programmes across departments including Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Statistics, Marine Biology, Atmospheric Sciences, and more.

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Qualifying Degree | B.Sc. (or B.Tech where applicable) in a relevant subject from a recognised university |
| Minimum Aggregate | 55% in the qualifying degree (50% for SC/ST candidates) |
| Subject Requirements | Vary by department; applicants must have studied the core subject at the undergraduate level |

<Accordion title="Are subject-specific prerequisites mandatory?">
  Yes. Each M.Sc. programme specifies which undergraduate subjects or disciplines are eligible. For instance, M.Sc. Physics typically requires a B.Sc. with Physics as a main subject. Check the department page or the prospectus for the exact list of eligible qualifying degrees.
</Accordion>

### MBA (Master of Business Administration)

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Qualifying Degree | Any Bachelor's degree (in any discipline) from a recognised university |
| Minimum Aggregate | 55% in the qualifying degree (50% for SC/ST) |
| Work Experience | Not mandatory for fresh graduates; check the current prospectus for any updates |

### MCA (Master of Computer Applications)

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Qualifying Degree | Any Bachelor's degree with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level |
| Minimum Aggregate | 55% in the qualifying degree (50% for SC/ST) |

## Doctoral Programmes

### PhD

CUSAT's doctoral programmes are open to candidates with a postgraduate degree in the relevant subject. Admission is through the **Doctoral Admission Test (DAT)**, which may be waived for NET/GATE/JRF holders in some departments.

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Qualifying Degree | Master's degree (M.Sc. / M.Tech / MBA or equivalent) in a relevant subject |
| Minimum Aggregate | 55% in the qualifying degree |
| Minimum Aggregate (SC/ST) | 50% in the qualifying degree |
| Entrance Test | DAT (Doctoral Admission Test), conducted department-wise |
| Exemptions | Candidates with UGC-NET / CSIR-NET / GATE / JRF may be exempted from DAT in some departments |

<Accordion title="Can I apply for a PhD while still completing my Master's degree?">
  Candidates in their final year of the qualifying Master's programme may apply provisionally. However, admission is confirmed only upon submission of the final degree certificate or consolidated mark sheet showing the required minimum percentage. Check the PhD admission notification from the relevant department for the specific deadline.
</Accordion>

## Reserved Category Relaxations

CUSAT implements the reservation policy of the Government of Kerala. Key relaxations for reserved categories include:

- **SC / ST candidates:** 5% relaxation in minimum aggregate marks across most programmes
- **Reserved seats:** A percentage of seats in every programme is reserved for SC, ST, OBC, EWS, and other notified categories
- **Special admissions:** SC/ST special admission rounds are conducted in addition to the regular centralised allotment, as mandated by statutory requirements
- **Age relaxation:** Upper age limit is relaxed for reserved categories as per government norms

<Info>
  The exact number of reserved seats per programme is listed in the programme-specific section of the official prospectus. Category certificates must be current and issued by a competent authority.
</Info>

## International and NRI Students

International students and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) are not required to appear for CUSAT CAT. Admissions for this group are handled separately through the **Office of International Relations (OIR)**. Visit [oir.cusat.ac.in](https://oir.cusat.ac.in) for eligibility norms, equivalent qualifications, and the application procedure.
