Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT) offers **24 undergraduate programmes** spanning Engineering, Technology, Marine Sciences, Law, Science, and Management. Whether you are interested in a four-year B.Tech, a five-year integrated law degree, or a foundational B.Sc. in natural sciences, CUSAT's undergraduate curriculum is designed to blend rigorous theory with hands-on application. Admissions to all UG programmes are conducted through the CUSAT Common Admission Test (CAT) or equivalent entrance examination. The complete and up-to-date list of programmes offered in the current academic year is published each year in the official prospectus at [admissions.cusat.ac.in](https://admissions.cusat.ac.in).

## Admission Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register on the Admissions Portal">
    Create your account at [admissions.cusat.ac.in](https://admissions.cusat.ac.in) and fill in the online application form. No offline forms are accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Appear for CUSAT CAT">
    Sit for the Common Admission Test (CAT) conducted by CUSAT. The test is programme-specific; make sure you register for the correct paper(s).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attend Allotment Rounds">
    Rank lists are published on the admissions portal. Participate in centralised allotment rounds and confirm your seat online within the stipulated time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report for Admission">
    Report to your allotted department with original certificates, migration certificate, and proof of fee payment to complete enrolment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The exact set of programmes offered, intake numbers, and eligibility criteria may change from year to year. Always refer to the current prospectus at [admissions.cusat.ac.in](https://admissions.cusat.ac.in) before applying.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If you are a foreign national or a Person of Indian Origin (PIO), you may not need to appear in CUSAT CAT. Contact the Office of International Relations at [oir.cusat.ac.in](https://oir.cusat.ac.in) or write to [oir@cusat.ac.in](mailto:oir@cusat.ac.in) for a dedicated admissions pathway.
</Tip>
