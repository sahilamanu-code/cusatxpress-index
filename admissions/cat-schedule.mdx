The CUSAT Common Admission Test follows a well-defined annual calendar that runs from the opening of the application window in January through to real-time admissions in August–September. Planning your preparation and application around this cycle is essential — missing a registration deadline or an allotment round can mean waiting a full year for the next opportunity. The dates below reflect the **typical approximate schedule**; the university publishes the exact, binding dates on its official admissions portal and news section at the start of each admission cycle.

<Warning>
  Dates vary from year to year and are subject to change. Always verify the official schedule at **admissions.cusat.ac.in** and **cusat.ac.in/news** before making any plans. Relying on dates from a previous year's cycle or third-party sources may cause you to miss critical deadlines.
</Warning>

## Annual CAT Calendar

The table below outlines each stage of the admissions process and its approximate window during the calendar year.

| Stage | Approximate Period |
|---|---|
| Online Application Opens | January – February |
| Last Date to Apply | February – March |
| Hall Ticket Download | 1–2 weeks before the exam |
| CAT Examination | April |
| Result / Rank List Publication | May |
| Centralised Allotment — Round 1 | May – June |
| Allotment Rounds 2 & 3 | June – July |
| Real-Time / Spot Admissions | August – September |
| Classes Begin (Odd Semester) | July |

## Stage-by-Stage Breakdown

### Online Application Window

The application portal at **admissions.cusat.ac.in** opens in January or February. You must register, complete your application form, upload documents, and pay the fee before the last date — typically in late February or March. There is no grace period once the portal closes.

<Tip>
  Apply as early as possible. Submitting your application well before the deadline gives you time to correct any errors, re-upload documents if rejected, and complete fee payment without last-minute technical issues.
</Tip>

### Hall Ticket Download

Hall tickets (admit cards) are made available for download on the admissions portal one to two weeks before the exam date. You cannot sit the exam without a printed or digital copy of your hall ticket. Ensure that all details — name, photograph, test centre, and date — are correct. Contact the Directorate of Admissions immediately if you spot any discrepancy.

### CAT Examination

The computer-based CUSAT CAT is held in April, usually across multiple sessions to accommodate applicants for different programmes. Test centres are located in Kochi and other notified cities across India. Your hall ticket specifies your assigned test centre, session, and reporting time. Arrive at the centre at least 30 minutes before your scheduled start time.

<Info>
  Candidates who apply for multiple programmes may be required to appear for more than one paper, which may be scheduled on different dates or sessions. Confirm your exam schedule on the hall ticket and the admissions portal.
</Info>

### Result and Rank List Publication

Results are processed and a programme-wise merit/rank list is published on the admissions portal in May. The rank list shows your rank within the general merit list as well as within your reserved category (if applicable). Only candidates on the rank list are eligible to participate in the allotment process.

### Centralised Allotment Process

CUSAT conducts centralised allotment (also called counselling) in multiple rounds, typically spanning May through July:

| Round | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Round 1 | First allotment based on rank and programme preferences submitted online |
| Round 2 | Re-allotment after candidates who did not accept Round 1 seats are removed; candidates can also upgrade |
| Round 3 | Final main allotment round; clears most remaining vacancies |

During each round, you submit your programme preferences online. Seats are allotted based on your rank, the preferences you indicate, category reservation, and seat availability. You must accept your allotted seat within the stipulated window, pay the confirmation fee, and submit documents — failing to do so forfeits your allotted seat.

<Accordion title="Can I change my programme preferences between rounds?">
  Yes. Between allotment rounds, you typically have a window to revise or rearrange your programme preferences. Candidates who received an allotment in Round 1 and want to be considered for a higher-preference programme can retain their existing allotment while competing for an upgrade in Round 2. The exact rules for preference revision are published in the allotment schedule notification.
</Accordion>

### Real-Time / Spot Admissions

After all three main allotment rounds are concluded, seats that remain vacant are filled through **real-time admissions** or **spot admissions**, usually held in August–September. Eligible candidates who appeared in the CAT and are still on the rank list attend the spot admission session in person at the university. Seats are offered in rank order on the day itself.

<Note>
  Spot admissions are conducted only for seats that are genuinely vacant after the main allotment process. The number of seats available and the eligible rank range are announced just before the session. Carry all original documents and fee payment proof when you attend.
</Note>

### Classes Begin

The odd semester (first semester for new students) generally commences in **July** for most programmes. Students who join through spot admissions in August–September may need to catch up on content from the initial weeks; departments typically provide guidance on this.

## PhD Doctoral Admission Test (DAT)

The PhD admissions cycle follows a slightly different timeline. After the main CAT results are published, each department conducts its own **Doctoral Admission Test (DAT)** along with a research aptitude interview. The DAT schedule is announced department-wise and is separate from the UG/PG CAT cycle. NET/GATE/JRF holders may be exempted from the DAT in certain departments — refer to the PhD admission notification from the relevant department for details.

1. **Bookmark** [admissions.cusat.ac.in](https://admissions.cusat.ac.in) and check it regularly from December onwards.
2. **Follow** [cusat.ac.in/news](https://cusat.ac.in/news) for official press releases and admission notifications.
3. **Register early** — once you create an account on the portal, you may receive email notifications about important dates.
