CUSAT's postgraduate landscape is one of the most diverse among technical universities in India, with **55 programmes** spread across engineering, pure sciences, marine sciences, management, law, and the humanities. The university offers 42 M.Tech specialisations and 48 M.Sc. programmes alongside a full-time MBA, a two-year LLM, and M.A. degrees in language studies. Admission to M.Tech programmes is primarily through GATE score or CUSAT CAT, while M.Sc., MBA, and other programmes use the CUSAT CAT. After each main allotment round, the university conducts **real-time / spot admission** sessions to fill any remaining vacant seats — watch the admissions portal for notifications.

<Accordion title="Management — MBA">
  The **School of Management Studies** offers a full-time two-year **MBA programme** with a focus on general management, finance, marketing, and human resources. The programme is designed for students and working professionals who wish to build leadership skills grounded in analytical thinking.

  - Admission: CUSAT CAT (Management paper) or equivalent score
  - Duration: Two years (four semesters)
  - Specialisations: Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management, Operations

  The **Centre for Budget Studies** at CUSAT also offers advanced opportunities in economic policy research for postgraduate students.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Law — LLM">
  The **School of Legal Studies** offers a two-year LLM programme for law graduates who wish to specialise and pursue academic or advanced legal careers.

  - Duration: Two years (four semesters)
  - Admission: CUSAT CAT (Law paper)
  - Focus areas include constitutional law, international law, commercial law, and human rights

  LLM graduates are eligible for PhD research at the School of Legal Studies and for faculty positions subject to UGC norms.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Humanities — M.A. in English & Hindi">
  The Departments of English & Foreign Languages and Hindi offer M.A. programmes for students with a strong interest in literature, linguistics, and cultural studies.

  - M.A. English (Dept. of English & Foreign Languages)
  - M.A. Hindi (Dept. of Hindi)

  Both departments also offer PhD programmes and have active research in comparative literature, translation studies, and language pedagogy.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Computer Applications — MCA">
  The **Department of Computer Applications** offers the Master of Computer Applications (MCA) programme, a three-year postgraduate degree focused on software development, algorithms, data science, and enterprise computing.

  - Duration: Three years (six semesters)
  - Admission: CUSAT CAT (Computer Applications paper)
</Accordion>

## Spot / Real-Time Admissions

<Info>
  After the main allotment rounds are completed, CUSAT conducts **real-time admissions** (also called spot admissions) to fill seats that remain vacant. Eligible candidates on the rank list who have not yet been allotted a seat are invited to participate. Notifications are published on [admissions.cusat.ac.in](https://admissions.cusat.ac.in) — check frequently during the admission season.
</Info>

## Admission Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply Online">
    Register and submit your application on [admissions.cusat.ac.in](https://admissions.cusat.ac.in). Upload all required documents, including mark sheets, eligibility certificates, and category certificates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Appear for CUSAT CAT or Submit GATE Score">
    M.Tech applicants may use a valid GATE score. For all other PG programmes, you must appear in the relevant CUSAT CAT paper.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Rank List & Participate in Allotment">
    Rank lists are published department-wise on the portal. Accept your allotment and pay the required seat-confirmation fee within the deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Admission Formalities">
    Report to your department with original documents and complete the admission process. Late reporting may result in forfeiture of the allotted seat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you hold a GATE score, you may be eligible for a **GATE scholarship** (JRF/SRF) while pursuing your M.Tech. Check with your department for scholarship details after joining.
</Tip>
