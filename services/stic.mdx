The **Sophisticated Test and Instrumentation Centre (STIC)** at CUSAT is a state-of-the-art facility that provides high-end analytical, testing, and calibration services to researchers, academic institutions, and industry clients. STIC holds **ISO/IEC 17025:2017 accreditation** from the National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories (NABL), which means every measurement and test report meets internationally recognised quality standards. Whether you are a CUSAT researcher characterising a new material or an external industry partner requiring certified testing, STIC's expert team and cutting-edge instrumentation are available to you.

<Info>
  STIC services are available to both CUSAT researchers and external clients from academic institutions, government agencies, and private industry. External clients should contact STIC directly to obtain a quotation and schedule instrument time.
</Info>

## Instruments and Services

<Accordion title="Electron Microscopy">
  High-resolution imaging and microstructural analysis of solid materials, thin films, nanoparticles, and biological specimens.

  - **Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM) with Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy (EDS)** — surface morphology imaging and elemental mapping
  - **High-Resolution Transmission Electron Microscope (HR-TEM)** — atomic-level lattice imaging, selected area electron diffraction (SAED)
  - **Ultra Microtome** — preparation of ultra-thin sections for TEM analysis of polymers, composites, and biological samples
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="X-Ray Diffraction">
  Crystallographic analysis and phase identification for powders, single crystals, and thin films.

  - **Powder X-Ray Diffractometer (PXRD)** — phase identification, crystallite size estimation (Scherrer equation), strain analysis, Rietveld refinement
  - **Single Crystal X-Ray Diffractometer** — complete 3D crystal structure determination
  - **Database & Software** — ICDD PDF database and structure refinement software for data analysis
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Elemental Analysis">
  Trace and bulk elemental composition determination across a wide range of matrices.

  - **ICP-Mass Spectrometer (ICP-MS)** — ultra-trace multi-element analysis in water, geological, biological, and environmental samples; detection limits in the parts-per-trillion range
  - **CHNS Analyzer** — simultaneous determination of carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, and sulfur content in organic and inorganic materials
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="NMR & Mass Spectrometry">
  Structural elucidation of organic, inorganic, and biological molecules.

  - **MALDI-TOF Mass Spectrometer** — molecular weight determination of polymers, proteins, peptides, and large biomolecules
  - **400 MHz NMR Spectrometer** — multinuclear solution-state NMR including:
    - ¹H and ¹³C standard spectra
    - DEPT (Distortionless Enhancement by Polarisation Transfer)
    - COSY (Correlation Spectroscopy)
    - HMQC (Heteronuclear Multiple-Quantum Coherence)
    - ³¹P and ¹⁵N NMR for phosphorus- and nitrogen-containing compounds
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Thermal Analysis">
  Characterisation of thermal stability, phase transitions, and heat flow in materials.

  - **Thermogravimetric Analyser with Differential Thermal Analysis (TGA-DTA)** — mass change and phase transitions from room temperature to **1500 °C**; suitable for polymers, ceramics, metals, and composites
  - **Differential Scanning Calorimeter (DSC)** — heat flow measurements from **−150 °C to 600 °C**; determination of melting point, glass transition temperature (Tg), crystallisation, and specific heat capacity
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Spectroscopy">
  Optical and vibrational spectroscopic analysis for a broad range of materials.

  - **UV-Vis-NIR Spectrophotometer** — absorbance, transmittance, and reflectance measurements from the ultraviolet through the near-infrared region; suitable for solutions, thin films, and solid powders
  - **Fourier Transform Infrared Spectrometer (FTIR) with ATR Accessory** — identification of functional groups and molecular structure; the Attenuated Total Reflectance (ATR) accessory allows direct analysis of liquids, pastes, and solid surfaces with minimal sample preparation
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Testing & Calibration Services">
  NABL-accredited testing and calibration covering environmental, electrical, and physical parameters.

  - **Water Quality Analysis** — physicochemical and heavy-metal analysis of drinking water, wastewater, and environmental water samples in compliance with IS/WHO standards
  - **Ion Chromatography** — quantification of anions (fluoride, chloride, nitrate, sulfate, phosphate) and cations in water and aqueous solutions
  - **Electro-technical Calibration** — calibration of voltmeters, ammeters, wattmeters, and other electrical measuring instruments
  - **Thermal Calibration** — calibration of thermometers, thermocouples, temperature loggers, and ovens
  - **Mechanical Calibration** — calibration of mass standards, pressure gauges, and dimensional measuring tools
  - **Electrical Testing** — testing of electrical equipment and components to relevant Indian and international standards
  - **Solar PV Testing** — performance and safety testing of solar photovoltaic panels and associated equipment
</Accordion>
