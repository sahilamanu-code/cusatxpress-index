Beyond its core academic programmes, CUSAT's departments and schools actively extend their expertise to industry, government bodies, and the wider community through consultancy, analytical testing, training workshops, and collaborative research. These services leverage the university's specialised laboratories, experienced faculty, and accredited testing infrastructure. Whether you need an environmental impact assessment, a polymer characterisation study, statistical data analysis, or specialist training in oceanographic data processing, the relevant CUSAT department can partner with you. Below is an overview of services offered by individual departments, along with direct contact information.

<Info>
  Service charges, timelines, and specific deliverables are negotiated directly with each department. Contact the listed faculty or departmental email to discuss your requirements and obtain a quotation.
</Info>

## Department-Wise Services

<Accordion title="Physical Oceanography">
  The Department of Physical Oceanography provides scientific services related to the sea, coast, and ocean data.

  **Services offered:**
  - Ocean services and oceanographic surveys
  - Coastal protection studies and shoreline change analysis
  - Bathymetry surveys (sea floor mapping)
  - Training in ocean data analysis using Python and MATLAB

  **Contact:** oceans@cusat.ac.in · **Phone:** 0484-2363950
</Accordion>
