The **eStudents Portal** at [estudents.cusat.ac.in](https://estudents.cusat.ac.in) is the single digital gateway through which every enrolled CUSAT student manages their academic life. From downloading your hall ticket before an examination to checking your semester results the moment they are published, everything you need is available around the clock — no queue, no paperwork. Log in using your university-issued CUSAT credentials (the same username and password you use for CUSAT Mail) and the dashboard surfaces only the services relevant to your programme and year.

## Accessing the Portal

To sign in, navigate to [estudents.cusat.ac.in](https://estudents.cusat.ac.in) and enter your **CUSAT Student ID** and **password**. If you are logging in for the first time or have forgotten your password, contact your department office or the university's IT helpdesk to have your credentials reset.

<Note>
  Your CUSAT login credentials are shared across the eStudents Portal, CUSAT Mail, and the Feedback System. Keep them secure and do not share them with anyone.
</Note>

## What You Can Do on the Portal

The eStudents Portal bundles several essential services into one authenticated session. Here is a breakdown of the key features available to you:

- **Admissions Details** — View your registered programme, specialisation, batch, and application records.
- **Hall Ticket Download** — Download your admit card before university examinations. Hall tickets are released a few days before the exam schedule begins; you will receive a notification on your CUSAT Mail when they are available.
- **Exam Results** — Check semester and supplementary examination results as soon as they are declared by the Controller of Examinations.
- **Academic Records** — Review your attendance, internal marks, and other academic data maintained by your school or department.

<Tip>
  Bookmark [estudents.cusat.ac.in](https://estudents.cusat.ac.in) on your phone's browser so you can access your hall ticket and results without having to search for the URL each time.
</Tip>
