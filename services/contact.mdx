Whether you need to speak with the Directorate of Admissions about your application, reach the Controller of Examinations about a result query, or contact the Security Office in an emergency, this page puts every important CUSAT contact in one place. The general enquiry lines are staffed between **10 AM and 5 PM** on working days; the Security Office operates **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**.

<Note>
  For student-specific online services — eStudents Portal, Exam Portal, fee payment, and grievances — see the dedicated pages in this section rather than calling the general lines.
</Note>

## When to Contact Which Office

- **Admissions queries** (application status, eligibility, document submission): Directorate of Admissions
- **Examination issues** (results, hall tickets, re-valuation, mark certificates): Controller of Examinations
- **International students and exchange programmes**: Office of International Relations
- **Fee receipts, financial aid disbursements, scholarship payments**: Finance Office
- **Employee and service matters, general administrative queries**: Registrar's Office
- **Security, lost items, campus emergency**: Security Office (24 hours)
- **Media enquiries, press releases**: Public Relations & Publications

<Warning>
  For urgent on-campus emergencies — medical, security, or fire — contact the Security Office immediately at **+91(484)2575101**. Do not wait to reach other offices.
</Warning>
