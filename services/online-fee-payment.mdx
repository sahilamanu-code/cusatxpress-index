CUSAT processes all student fee payments — semester fees, examination fees, hostel rent, and miscellaneous charges — through the **SBI Collect** platform, a secure online payment gateway operated by the State Bank of India. Before you begin, make sure you have your student registration number, fee challan details (if applicable), and the correct **Corporation ID** for your specific payment category. Using the wrong Corporation ID is the single most common cause of payment mismatches, so read the table below carefully before you proceed.

## Corporation IDs at a Glance

| Payment Type | Corporation ID |
|---|---|
| Semester Fees – School of Engineering | **591259** |
| Examination & Other Fees – School of Engineering | **604885** |
| Hostel Rent | **591259** |
| Centre for Integrated Studies | **1805756** |

<Warning>
  Always double-check that you have selected the correct Corporation ID before confirming your payment. Using the wrong ID may credit your payment to the wrong account, and the reversal process can take several weeks. If you are unsure, contact your department office or the Finance Office at **+91(484)2576107** before paying.
</Warning>

## Step-by-Step Payment Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open SBI Collect">
    Go to [sbi.co.in/sbicollect](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/sbicollect/icollecthome.htm) in your browser, or search for **"SBI Collect"** on the SBI homepage at sbi.co.in and follow the link in the navigation menu. You do not need an SBI bank account to make a payment — most debit/credit cards and UPI are accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Payment Category">
    On the SBI Collect home page, accept the terms and conditions, then choose **"Educational Institutions"** from the category drop-down. In the next field, select **"Kerala"** as the state and search for **"Cochin University of Science and Technology"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Correct Corporation ID">
    You will be prompted to enter or select a Corporation ID. Refer to the table above and enter the ID that matches your payment type. Confirm the institution name displayed on screen matches **Cochin University of Science and Technology** before moving forward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill In Your Student Details">
    Complete the payment form with the following information:
    - Your full name (as registered with the university)
    - Your student registration / roll number
    - The academic year and semester
    - The exact fee amount as notified by your department or fee notification letter
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Payment Method and Pay">
    Select your preferred payment method — internet banking, debit card, credit card, or UPI — and complete the transaction. Do not close the browser tab until you see the **payment success confirmation** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Receipt">
    Once the payment is successful, note down the **SBI Collect Reference Number** (also called the DU number) displayed on the confirmation screen. Download or screenshot the receipt. You will need this reference number if you are asked to produce proof of payment by your department or the Finance Office.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Send the payment receipt to your registered CUSAT Mail inbox immediately after the transaction by using the "Send Receipt by Email" option on the SBI Collect confirmation page. This gives you a permanent, searchable record.
</Tip>

## Other Fee Payment Options

For fee categories not covered by the Corporation IDs listed above — such as departmental fees, library fines, or special examination charges — visit the **Online Payment** section linked in the footer of the CUSAT official website at [cusat.ac.in](https://cusat.ac.in). That page lists additional payment routes and updated fee schedules.

<Info>
  If your payment is deducted from your bank account but no confirmation is received on SBI Collect, wait 24 hours before raising a complaint. Most pending transactions are automatically reconciled within one business day. If the issue persists, contact SBI Collect customer care and copy the CUSAT Finance Office at **+91(484)2576107**.
</Info>
