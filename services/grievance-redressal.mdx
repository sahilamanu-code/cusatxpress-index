CUSAT is committed to ensuring that every student has a fair, accessible, and confidential mechanism to voice concerns — whether they relate to academics, administration, examination, campus life, or personal safety. You are encouraged to resolve issues at the earliest opportunity using the most appropriate channel. This page describes all the official grievance redressal pathways available to you, from the university's own portal to the national UGC platform and the independent Ombudsperson.

<Info>
  Grievances raised through official channels are treated with confidentiality. You will not face any academic or administrative disadvantage for filing a legitimate, good-faith complaint.
</Info>

## Grievance Channels

### Grievance Redressal Portal

The primary channel for academic and administrative complaints is the **CUSAT Student Grievance Redressal Portal** at [cusat.ac.in/student/grievances](https://cusat.ac.in/student/grievances). You can log in with your student credentials, submit a grievance, track its status, and receive responses from the relevant authority — all in one place.

**Suitable for:**
- Examination-related issues (results, re-valuation, hall ticket problems)
- Attendance and internal mark disputes
- Administrative delays or fee-related problems
- Library, hostel, or campus facility complaints

### Ombudsperson

### Students Union

The **CUSAT Students Union** at [studentsunion.cusat.ac.in](https://studentsunion.cusat.ac.in) represents the student body and can advocate on your behalf for collective or individual concerns. If you feel more comfortable raising an issue through a peer representative before approaching university administration, the Students Union is your first stop.

### UGC e-Samadhan

If your grievance remains unresolved after exhausting CUSAT's internal mechanisms, you can escalate it to the **University Grants Commission** through the national **e-Samadhan** portal at [samadhaan.ugc.ac.in](https://samadhaan.ugc.ac.in). This portal accepts grievances from students at any UGC-recognised university in India and is monitored by the UGC directly.

<Tip>
  Before submitting on e-Samadhan, keep a record of the reference numbers from your CUSAT portal complaint and any written correspondence you have had with the department or administration. This significantly speeds up the UGC review process.
</Tip>

## Choosing the Right Channel

| Situation | Recommended Channel |
|---|---|
| Result error or attendance dispute | Grievance Redressal Portal |
| Unresolved institutional complaint | Ombudsperson |
| Collective student concern | Students Union |
| Complaint not resolved by university | UGC e-Samadhan |
| Sexual harassment or gender issue | Women Helpline / ICC via Portal |

<Warning>
  Frivolous or false complaints made with malicious intent may attract disciplinary action under university regulations. Always provide accurate and complete information when filing a grievance.
</Warning>
