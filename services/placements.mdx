CUSAT takes an active role in bridging the gap between academic learning and professional careers. Through a network of dedicated offices — the Central Placement Office, the Employment Bureau, the CITTIC innovation hub, and the Equal Opportunity Cell — the university provides students with campus recruitment opportunities, entrepreneurship incubation, government employment registration, and targeted career development support. Whether your goal is to join a multinational firm, launch a startup, or secure a government position, CUSAT has a pathway designed to help you get there.

## Central Placement Office (CPO)

The **Central Placement Office** is the primary interface between CUSAT students and prospective employers. CPO coordinates on-campus and off-campus recruitment drives, maintains relationships with hiring companies, and publishes placement-related notices and schedules.

**Portal:** [cpo.cusat.ac.in](https://cpo.cusat.ac.in)

Through CPO you can:
- Register your profile for campus placements
- Access pre-placement talk schedules and company profiles
- Track placement drives, shortlists, and offer letters
- Find resources for interview preparation and aptitude practice

<Tip>
  Create your CPO profile as early as possible — ideally in your second year — so that you appear in the database when companies submit their eligibility criteria ahead of placement season.
</Tip>

## CITTIC — Centre for Innovation, Technology Transfer and Industrial Collaboration

**[cittic.cusat.ac.in](https://cittic.cusat.ac.in)**

CITTIC is CUSAT's entrepreneurship and technology transfer hub. If you have a business idea, a research prototype you want to commercialise, or a startup you are trying to grow, CITTIC offers the resources and mentoring to take you from concept to company.

**What CITTIC offers:**
- Startup incubation space and co-working facilities
- Mentoring by industry experts and serial entrepreneurs
- Access to funding networks, angel investors, and grant schemes
- Technology transfer support for faculty and student innovations
- Industry collaboration and MoU facilitation

## CUSATECH Foundation

The **CUSATECH Foundation** operates the **Technology Business Incubator (TBI)** and the **Employability & Innovation Hub** at CUSAT. These programmes focus on bridging the gap between cutting-edge university research and market-ready products, while also building the employability skills of graduating students through workshops, hackathons, industry exposure visits, and certification programmes.

## Employment Bureau

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Employment Bureau" icon="briefcase">
    Established on **10 January 1973** as a joint venture between CUSAT and the **National Employment Service, Kerala**, the Employment Bureau assists students and alumni in registering with government employment exchanges and accessing notifications for public-sector jobs, PSU recruitment, and government-assisted schemes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recruitment Portal" icon="magnifying-glass" href="https://recruit.cusat.ac.in">
    The portal at **recruit.cusat.ac.in** publishes faculty and staff vacancies at CUSAT. Reviewing this portal also gives students insight into the skills and qualifications currently sought by the university and affiliated institutions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  If you are a final-year student or a recent graduate, visit the Employment Bureau in person to register your name and qualifications. This registration is a prerequisite for certain government job applications and scholarship schemes in Kerala.
</Info>

## Equal Opportunity Cell

The **Equal Opportunity Cell (EOC)** at [eocsoce.cusat.ac.in](https://eocsoce.cusat.ac.in) specifically supports students from Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), and other underrepresented communities in accessing career opportunities. The Cell provides:

- Career counselling and guidance sessions
- Coaching for competitive examinations (UPSC, PSC, banking)
- Information on government scholarships and reservations
- Facilitation of campus placement registration for eligible students

## Strengthen Your Profile with Online Certifications

Employers increasingly value verifiable online credentials. You can boost your placement profile by completing courses on:

- **NPTEL** — technical and science courses from IITs and IISc, with proctored certification exams held on campus
- **SWAYAM** — UGC-approved MOOCs covering a broad range of subjects; credits may be transferable to your degree
- **Coursera / edX / LinkedIn Learning** — industry-aligned certifications in data science, machine learning, business analytics, and more

<Note>
  Some NPTEL certifications carry additional credit marks that are factored into your CGPA under CUSAT's credit transfer policy. Check with your department academic council for the current list of approved NPTEL courses.
</Note>
