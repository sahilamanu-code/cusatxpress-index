CUSAT's engineering schools form the backbone of technical education at the university, spanning civil, mechanical, electrical, electronics, computer, safety, and marine disciplines. Whether you are pursuing an undergraduate B.Tech, a postgraduate M.Tech, or a doctoral research degree, you will find a programme shaped by decades of academic rigour and industry relevance. All engineering programmes offered under the School of Engineering hold **NBA Tier I reaccreditation valid until December 2028**.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="School of Engineering (SOE)" icon="building-columns">
    The School of Engineering is CUSAT's flagship engineering faculty, housed on the main Kalamassery campus. It offers B.Tech, M.Tech, and PhD programmes across six departments:

    - **Civil Engineering** — structural design, environmental engineering, transportation
    - **Mechanical Engineering** — thermal systems, manufacturing, CAD/CAM
    - **Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)** — power systems, control engineering
    - **Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)** — VLSI, signal processing, wireless communication
    - **Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)** — algorithms, networks, machine learning
    - **Safety and Fire Engineering** — industrial safety, fire protection systems

    Research at SOE spans materials science, smart grids, embedded systems, and structural health monitoring, with strong ties to PSUs and defence organisations in the Cochin region.
  </Card>

  <Card title="CUCEK — Kuttanad Campus" icon="water">
    The **Cochin University College of Engineering, Kuttanad (CUCEK)** is located at Pulincunnu in the backwater-rich Kuttanad region of Alappuzha district. This campus extends CUSAT's engineering reach into one of Kerala's most ecologically significant landscapes.

    CUCEK offers B.Tech programmes in core engineering disciplines, giving students the same curriculum quality as the main campus while benefiting from a unique environment that fosters research in water resources, flood management, and sustainable construction.

    The campus is well connected by road and water transport, and its location makes it especially suited for civil and environmental engineering field work.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Kunjali Marakkar School of Marine Engineering" icon="anchor">
    Named after the legendary Kerala naval commander Kunjali Marakkar, this school is dedicated to marine engineering education and research. It offers B.Tech, M.Tech, and PhD programmes with a curriculum designed to meet the demands of the global maritime industry.

    Key areas of study include:
    - Marine propulsion systems
    - Ship machinery and automation
    - Offshore engineering
    - Naval architecture fundamentals

    The school collaborates closely with the School of Marine Sciences and the Department of Ship Technology, giving students access to interdisciplinary maritime expertise unmatched in the region.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Department of Ship Technology" icon="ship">
    Operating under the Technology faculty, the Department of Ship Technology complements marine engineering with a focus on naval architecture and ocean engineering. It offers B.Tech, M.Tech, and PhD degrees.

    **Research specialisations include:**
    - Marine Hydrodynamics
    - Ship Structural Design
    - Offshore Structures
    - Computational Fluid Dynamics for marine applications

    Graduates from this department are sought after by shipyards, classification societies, and offshore oil and gas companies worldwide.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Admission and Eligibility

Admission to B.Tech programmes is through **KEAM** (Kerala Engineering Architecture Medical entrance) for Kerala candidates, and through the CUSAT Common Admission Test (CAT) for non-Kerala and NRI candidates. M.Tech admissions use GATE scores. PhD admissions are conducted by the university twice a year.
