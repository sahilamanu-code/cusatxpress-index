CUSAT's social sciences, humanities, and law schools demonstrate that a science and technology university can nurture equally outstanding programmes in management, economics, legal studies, and languages. These schools and departments draw students seeking rigorous academic foundations alongside professional qualifications, and several of them carry national historical significance as pioneer institutions in their fields. Whether you aim for a career in law, management, public policy, or language education, CUSAT offers structured pathways from undergraduate to doctoral level.

## School of Management Studies

The School of Management Studies (SMS) at CUSAT holds a distinguished place in Indian management education — it is one of the **five original premier management schools in India**, established in **1964**, well before management education became widespread across the country. It offers **MBA** and **PhD** programmes.

The school combines classical management theory with Kerala's strong co-operative and maritime economic traditions, producing graduates who have risen to leadership positions in public sector enterprises, multinational corporations, and government agencies.

**Programme highlights:**
- MBA with elective streams in Finance, Marketing, HR, and Operations
- PhD in Management with specialisations in organisational behaviour, strategy, and financial management
- Active industry interface through guest lectures, summer internships, and live projects

<Info>
  The School of Management Studies is among the oldest management institutions in India. Its alumni network spans senior positions in government, PSUs, and the private sector across Kerala and beyond.
</Info>

## Department of Applied Economics

The Department of Applied Economics offers **MA** and **PhD** programmes, training economists to analyse real-world problems with rigorous quantitative and qualitative tools.

**Research areas:**
- Regional Development and Planning
- Industrial Economics and Competitiveness
- Fishery Economics and Natural Resource Management
- Poverty, Labour Markets, and Social Policy

The department's emphasis on applied work means you engage with data from Kerala's economy — its fisheries, tourism, remittance flows, and industrial estates — giving your research immediate relevance.

## Centre for Budget Studies (CBS)

The **Centre for Budget Studies (CBS)** is a research unit attached to the social sciences faculty that focuses on fiscal policy analysis, public expenditure review, and budget transparency. The centre undertakes policy-oriented research and engages with government bodies, civil society organisations, and academic networks working on fiscal accountability.

## DDU Kaushal Kendras (DDUKK)

The **DDU Kaushal Kendras (DDUKK)** are skill development centres operating under the Deen Dayal Upadhyaya scheme, providing vocational and employability training to students and community members. These centres bridge academic education and market-ready skills, offering short-term courses aligned with industry requirements.

## School of Legal Studies

Established in **1962**, the School of Legal Studies is one of the oldest law schools in Kerala and a founding institution of legal education in the state. It offers a comprehensive range of law programmes:

| Programme | Duration | Description |
|---|---|---|
| BBA LLB | 5 years | Integrated business law degree |
| B.Com LLB | 5 years | Integrated commerce and law degree |
| B.Sc. LLB | 5 years | Integrated science and law degree |
| LLM | 1 year | Postgraduate specialisation in law |
| PhD | — | Doctoral research in legal studies |

The school has a dedicated moot court, law library, and legal aid clinic, giving you practical exposure alongside academic instruction. Its location in Kochi — a major commercial and maritime hub — provides excellent access to courts, law firms, and corporate legal departments.

## Inter University Centre for IPR Studies

The **Inter University Centre for IPR Studies** is a collaborative research and education centre dedicated to Intellectual Property Rights (IPR). It offers academic programmes, certificate courses, and research on patents, trademarks, copyright, and technology transfer. Given CUSAT's strong science and technology base, this centre serves as a bridge between innovation and legal protection.

<Tip>
  If you are a science or engineering student, the Inter University Centre for IPR Studies offers short courses on patent filing and technology commercialisation that are highly valuable for research scholars and entrepreneurs.
</Tip>

## Department of Hindi

The Department of Hindi offers **MA** and **PhD** programmes in Hindi language and literature. It also fulfils the university's obligation under the Official Languages Act by promoting Hindi education across campus. The department organises cultural events, literary competitions, and workshops that celebrate Hindi literature and its connections to India's broader cultural heritage.
