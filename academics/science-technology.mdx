CUSAT's Science and Technology faculties bring together some of Kerala's most active research communities in areas ranging from artificial intelligence and photonics to nanomaterials and mathematical finance. You will find both professional degree programmes and deep research traditions, with departments that have been producing PhD graduates and publishing internationally for decades. The sections below cover each department's programmes, research strengths, and what makes it distinctive.

## Technology Departments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Department of Computer Applications" icon="laptop-code">
    The Department of Computer Applications offers the **MCA (Master of Computer Applications)** and a vibrant **PhD** programme. It is one of the leading MCA departments in South India, with a strong placement record and an active research culture.

    **Research areas:**
    - Multimedia Computing
    - Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
    - Software Engineering and Quality Assurance
    - Network Security and Distributed Systems

    The department maintains well-equipped computing labs and encourages students to participate in national-level hackathons and research competitions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Computer Science" icon="microchip">
    The Department of Computer Science focuses on advanced computing at the postgraduate and research levels, offering **M.Tech** and **PhD** programmes. It attracts students seeking specialisation in theory-intensive and systems-oriented computing.

    **Research areas:**
    - Natural Language Processing (NLP)
    - Distributed Computing
    - Computer Networks and Security
    - Algorithm Design and Computational Complexity

    Faculty here have extensive collaborations with national research institutions including IITs and ISRO.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Electronics" icon="circuit-board">
    One of CUSAT's oldest technology departments, the Department of Electronics offers both **B.Tech** and **M.Tech** degrees, making it one of the few departments in the Technology faculty with an undergraduate programme.

    **Research areas:**
    - Microwave Antennas and RF Engineering
    - Digital Signal Processing (DSP)
    - Microcontrollers and Embedded Systems
    - VLSI Design

    The department is equipped with advanced RF labs, signal processing workstations, and fabrication facilities, supporting both coursework and cutting-edge research.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Instrumentation" icon="gauge">
    The Department of Instrumentation offers **B.Tech** and **M.Tech** programmes with a focus on measurement science, control systems, and sensor technologies.

    **Research areas:**
    - PC-Based Instrumentation
    - Ultrasonic Sensing and Non-Destructive Testing
    - Neural Networks for Instrumentation
    - Biomedical Instrumentation

    Students benefit from close industry linkages with Kerala's industrial estates and research ties to healthcare technology organisations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="International School of Photonics" icon="lightbulb">
    The International School of Photonics is a nationally recognised centre of excellence for photonics research and education, offering **M.Sc.** and **PhD** programmes. It attracts students from across India and abroad.

    **Research areas:**
    - Laser Technology and Applications
    - Fiber Optic Sensors
    - Biophotonics and Biomedical Optics
    - Nonlinear Optics

    The school is equipped with state-of-the-art laser laboratories and has numerous active international collaborations, making it one of the most vibrant photonics research centres in India.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Polymer Science and Rubber Technology" icon="flask">
    Established in **1971**, the Department of Polymer Science and Rubber Technology is one of the pioneering departments in this field in India. It offers **B.Tech**, **M.Tech**, and **PhD** programmes.

    **Research areas:**
    - Nanomaterials and Polymer Nanocomposites
    - Biomaterials and Drug Delivery
    - Conducting Polymers
    - Rubber Technology and Compounding

    Given Kerala's significance in natural rubber production, this department holds a uniquely strategic position, with close ties to the Rubber Board of India and numerous rubber-based industries.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Ship Technology" icon="ship">
    The Department of Ship Technology offers **B.Tech**, **M.Tech**, and **PhD** programmes in naval architecture and ocean engineering, preparing you for careers in shipbuilding, offshore platforms, and marine consultancy.

    **Research areas:**
    - Marine Hydrodynamics
    - Ship Structural Design and Fatigue Analysis
    - Offshore Engineering
    - Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) for Ships

    The department has strong ties with Cochin Shipyard, the Indian Register of Shipping, and international maritime organisations.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Science Departments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Department of Applied Chemistry" icon="atom">
    The Department of Applied Chemistry offers **M.Sc.** and **PhD** programmes with a focus on applied and interdisciplinary chemical research relevant to industry and the environment.

    **Research areas:**
    - Electrochemical Sensors and Biosensors
    - Photochemistry and Photocatalysis
    - Green Catalysis and Sustainable Chemistry
    - Corrosion Science

    The department has well-equipped electrochemistry and spectroscopy laboratories and regularly publishes in high-impact international journals.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Biotechnology" icon="dna">
    Established in **1991**, the Department of Biotechnology has awarded approximately **75 PhDs** and maintains an active research culture. It offers **M.Sc.** and **PhD** programmes.

    **Research areas:**
    - Plant Biotechnology and Tissue Culture
    - Molecular Neurosciences
    - Microbial Biotechnology
    - Bioinformatics and Computational Biology

    The department benefits from CUSAT's proximity to marine and ecological resources, enabling distinctive research in aquatic and tropical biotechnology.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Mathematics" icon="square-root-variable">
    The Department of Mathematics offers **M.Sc.** and **PhD** programmes with strong foundations in pure and applied mathematics.

    **Research areas:**
    - Operator Theory and Functional Analysis
    - Fluid Mechanics and Computational Mathematics
    - Mathematical Finance and Actuarial Science
    - Graph Theory and Combinatorics

    The department has produced numerous faculty members serving in universities across India and maintains active collaborations with engineering and physics departments for applied projects.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Physics" icon="telescope">
    One of CUSAT's most productive research departments, Physics has awarded approximately **150 PhDs** and produced over **1,200 publications**. It offers **M.Sc.** and **PhD** programmes.

    **Research areas:**
    - Material Science and Condensed Matter Physics
    - Laser Physics and Spectroscopy
    - Nanostructures and Nanophotonics
    - Semiconductor Device Physics

    The department houses sophisticated characterisation facilities including XRD, SEM, and optical spectroscopy systems, and has strong national and international research collaborations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Statistics" icon="chart-bar">
    The Department of Statistics offers **M.Sc.** and **PhD** programmes, training statisticians for academia, government agencies, and the financial sector.

    **Research areas:**
    - Distribution Theory and Statistical Inference
    - Reliability Theory and Survival Analysis
    - Time Series Analysis
    - Bayesian Statistics

    Students here benefit from collaborations with CUSAT's economics, marine sciences, and engineering departments, gaining exposure to applied statistical problems across diverse domains.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Centre for Integrated Studies" icon="graduation-cap">
    The Centre for Integrated Studies offers a **5-year Integrated M.Sc.** programme, allowing you to complete both undergraduate and postgraduate studies seamlessly. This programme is ideal if you wish to pursue research at the highest level and want a deep, structured foundation in the sciences.

    The integrated approach removes the discontinuity of switching institutions between undergraduate and postgraduate studies, giving you continuity of mentorship and early exposure to research methodology.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
