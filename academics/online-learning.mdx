Being enrolled at CUSAT gives you access to a rich array of free and subsidised online learning resources — from MATLAB's full professional toolbox suite to government MOOC platforms and CUSAT's own e-content library. These platforms complement your classroom learning, support self-paced skill development, and help you prepare for competitive examinations, industry certifications, and research work. Make the most of every resource listed here; most are available at no additional cost as part of your enrolment.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="moodle@CUSAT" icon="chalkboard-user" href="https://moodle.cusat.ac.in">
    **moodle@CUSAT** is the university's official Learning Management System (LMS), powered by Moodle. It is the central hub for online courses offered by CUSAT departments.

    You can use Moodle to:
    - Access course materials, lecture slides, and assignments
    - Submit assignments and receive feedback online
    - Participate in discussion forums with classmates and faculty
    - Take online quizzes and internal assessments

    Log in with your CUSAT student credentials at [moodle.cusat.ac.in](https://moodle.cusat.ac.in).
  </Card>

  <Card title="MATLAB@CUSAT" icon="calculator" href="https://in.mathworks.com/academia/tah-portal/cochin-university-of-science-and-technology-40593370.html">
    CUSAT holds a **Total Academic Headcount (TAH) license** from MathWorks, giving every enrolled student and faculty member full access to **MATLAB, Simulink**, and a comprehensive suite of toolboxes — at no personal cost.

    This license includes toolboxes for signal processing, image processing, control systems, statistics, deep learning, and more. You can install MATLAB on your personal laptop or use it through the browser-based MATLAB Online.

    Activate your access through the MathWorks TAH portal linked to your CUSAT email address.
  </Card>

  <Card title="NPTEL" icon="video" href="https://nptel.ac.in">
    The **National Programme on Technology Enhanced Learning (NPTEL)** is a joint initiative of the IITs and IISc, offering thousands of free video lecture courses across engineering, sciences, humanities, and management.

    As a CUSAT student, you can:
    - Enrol in NPTEL courses for free
    - Appear for proctored certification exams for a nominal fee
    - Earn NPTEL certificates that are recognised by industry and academia

    Many CUSAT programmes allow NPTEL credits to be counted toward your CBCS elective requirements — check with your department.

    🔗 [nptel.ac.in](https://nptel.ac.in)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Spoken Tutorial" icon="microphone" href="https://spoken-tutorial.org">
    **Spoken Tutorial**, developed by IIT Bombay and funded by the National Mission on Education through ICT, provides free audio-visual tutorials on software tools, programming languages, and technical skills.

    Topics covered include:
    - Python, C, C++, Java, and Scilab programming
    - LibreOffice and LaTeX for document preparation
    - Linux command-line tools
    - FOSS tools for engineering and science

    Courses are available in multiple Indian languages, making them accessible to students from diverse linguistic backgrounds.

    🔗 [spoken-tutorial.org](https://spoken-tutorial.org)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Swayam" icon="graduation-cap" href="https://swayam.gov.in">
    **Swayam** is India's national MOOC platform, launched by the Ministry of Education. It hosts hundreds of courses from universities and institutions across India, covering undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional topics.

    Key features:
    - Free enrolment for all courses
    - Proctored examinations for credit transfer (minimal fee)
    - UGC-approved credit transfer framework
    - Courses from IITs, IIMs, NITs, and central universities

    CUSAT's CBCS framework supports credit transfer from Swayam courses — consult your academic advisor for details.

    🔗 [swayam.gov.in](https://swayam.gov.in)
  </Card>

  <Card title="CDeC — e-Content Centre" icon="film" href="https://cdec.cusat.ac.in">
    The **Centre for Development of e-Content (CDeC)** is CUSAT's in-house studio and production unit for digital academic content. It produces video lectures, animated explanations, and multimedia modules created by CUSAT faculty.

    CDeC content is particularly valuable because it is tailored to CUSAT's own syllabi and assessment patterns. You will find recordings of lectures by the very faculty who teach your courses.

    🔗 [cdec.cusat.ac.in](https://cdec.cusat.ac.in)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Digital Library (DSpace)" icon="database" href="https://dspace.cusat.ac.in">
    CUSAT's **DSpace Digital Library** is not just a repository — it is also a learning resource. It gives you access to:

    - PhD theses from CUSAT across all disciplines
    - Faculty research publications and reports
    - Course materials and technical documents
    - Dissertations you can study as research writing models

    Use DSpace at the start of any research project or advanced coursework module.

    🔗 [dspace.cusat.ac.in](https://dspace.cusat.ac.in)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Cyber Hygiene Handbook" icon="shield-halved" href="https://cusat.ac.in/uploads/Handbook_Cyber_Hygiene.pdf">
    CUSAT provides a free **Handbook on Cyber Hygiene** to all students, covering safe online practices essential in today's digital learning and research environment.

    Topics include:
    - Password security and two-factor authentication
    - Safe use of public Wi-Fi networks
    - Recognising phishing and social engineering attacks
    - Data privacy and responsible use of online platforms

    Download it free at [cusat.ac.in/uploads/Handbook\_Cyber\_Hygiene.pdf](https://cusat.ac.in/uploads/Handbook_Cyber_Hygiene.pdf).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Knowledge Forum

The **Knowledge Forum** is CUSAT's regular technical talk series, where faculty members from across the university deliver presentations on their research and areas of expertise. It is held on the **second Wednesday of every month**.

These sessions are open to all enrolled students and research scholars, and they offer:
- Exposure to cutting-edge research happening within your own university
- Networking opportunities with faculty from other departments
- Inspiration and ideas for your own research or project work
- A platform to learn about interdisciplinary connections between fields

<Tip>
  Attending Knowledge Forum sessions regularly is one of the best free ways to stay intellectually engaged outside your coursework. Keep an eye on the CUSAT notice boards and the official website for monthly session announcements.
</Tip>
