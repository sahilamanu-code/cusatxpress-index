CUSAT's library ecosystem is one of the most comprehensive among Indian universities, reflecting the institution's long-standing commitment to research across science, technology, engineering, marine sciences, law, and management. You have access to both physical collections and a suite of digital platforms that make research resources available from anywhere. Whether you need a doctoral thesis from the 1970s, a recent journal article, or a WHO technical report, the CUSAT library network has you covered.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="University Library" icon="building-columns" href="https://library.cusat.ac.in">
    The University Library at the main Kalamassery campus is recognised as one of the **best scientific and technical libraries in India**. It holds an extensive collection of books, journals, bound periodicals, conference proceedings, and reference works spanning all disciplines taught and researched at CUSAT.

    **Special Collections Section** houses materials of particular research and archival value:
    - Doctoral theses from CUSAT and affiliated institutions
    - Patents and patent gazettes
    - WHO and UNO technical documents and reports
    - Census reports and demographic data
    - Rare art books and illustrated volumes

    The library subscribes to major international journal databases and provides in-person and remote access to enrolled students and registered research scholars.

    🔗 [library.cusat.ac.in](https://library.cusat.ac.in)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Digital Library / DSpace" icon="database" href="https://dspace.cusat.ac.in">
    The **Digital Library of CUSAT**, built on the DSpace platform, is the primary institutional repository for CUSAT's digitised academic output. It provides open and authenticated access to:

    - Digitised theses and dissertations
    - Faculty publications and preprints
    - Conference papers and technical reports
    - Course materials and lecture notes

    DSpace allows you to search, browse, and download documents directly from your browser without needing to visit the physical library. It is the recommended starting point for literature surveys and thesis background research.

    🔗 [dspace.cusat.ac.in](https://dspace.cusat.ac.in)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Dyuthi Digital Repository" icon="archive" href="https://dyuthi.cusat.ac.in">
    **Dyuthi** (meaning "radiance" in Malayalam) is CUSAT's dedicated open-access digital repository. It is a curated collection of the university's intellectual output, made freely available to the global research community.

    Dyuthi contains:
    - PhD theses submitted to CUSAT
    - Research articles authored by CUSAT faculty
    - Technical reports from university-funded projects
    - Dissertations and project reports

    Dyuthi is indexed by international academic search engines, ensuring that CUSAT's research reaches a worldwide audience. As a researcher, depositing your work in Dyuthi increases its visibility and citation potential.

    🔗 [dyuthi.cusat.ac.in](https://dyuthi.cusat.ac.in)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Media Repository" icon="photo-film" href="https://media.cusat.ac.in">
    The **Media Repository** is CUSAT's digital archive for audio-visual academic content. It hosts recorded lectures, documentary material, educational videos, and multimedia resources produced by CUSAT's Centre for Development of e-Content (CDeC) and individual faculty members.

    The repository is especially useful if you missed a lecture, want to revisit a recorded seminar, or are looking for supplementary explanatory content for complex subjects.

    🔗 [media.cusat.ac.in](https://media.cusat.ac.in)
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Marine Science Library — Lakeside Campus

The **Lakeside Campus** of CUSAT, home to the School of Marine Sciences, maintains a dedicated marine science library that is regarded as **one of the largest marine science libraries in Asia**. This collection is a specialised resource for students and researchers in oceanography, marine biology, atmospheric sciences, fisheries, and marine geology.

The marine science library holds:
- Rare oceanographic survey reports and expedition logs
- International maritime and oceanographic journals (print and digital)
- Charts, bathymetric maps, and geological data sets
- FAO fisheries publications and UNEP marine environment reports
- Theses from India's leading marine science programmes

<Info>
  Access to physical collections at both the main campus library and the Lakeside Campus marine science library requires a valid CUSAT student identity card. Research scholars should register with the library at the start of each semester to activate database access.
</Info>

## Using Library Resources Remotely

You can access CUSAT's digital library resources from off-campus using your university credentials. The following platforms are available remotely:

| Platform | URL | Access Type |
|---|---|---|
| University Library Portal | [library.cusat.ac.in](https://library.cusat.ac.in) | Login required |
| DSpace Digital Library | [dspace.cusat.ac.in](https://dspace.cusat.ac.in) | Open + Login |
| Dyuthi Repository | [dyuthi.cusat.ac.in](https://dyuthi.cusat.ac.in) | Open Access |
| Media Repository | [media.cusat.ac.in](https://media.cusat.ac.in) | Login required |

<Tip>
  If you are working on your thesis or a research paper, start your literature review on Dyuthi and DSpace for CUSAT-specific research, then use the University Library's journal database subscriptions for international publications. This two-step approach is efficient and ensures you do not miss locally relevant prior work.
</Tip>
