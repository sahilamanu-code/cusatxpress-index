CUSAT organises its academic year into two semesters, giving you a clear and predictable schedule for coursework, examinations, and breaks. Understanding the semester structure helps you plan your studies, register for courses on time, and make the most of the vacation period for internships, research, or personal development. The university publishes a detailed academic calendar each year covering all key dates — class commencements, internal assessment schedules, examination dates, and holidays.

## Semester Schedule

| Semester | Period | Examinations |
|---|---|---|
| Odd Semester | July – October | November |
| Even Semester | December – March | April / May |
| Vacation | May – June | — |

<Note>
  The CUSAT academic calendar is published annually and is subject to revision based on university decisions, public holidays, and unforeseen circumstances. Always refer to the latest published calendar before making travel or registration plans.
</Note>

## Key Phases of the Academic Year

**July — Odd Semester begins.** New students complete their orientation and registration during the first two weeks. Continuing students resume coursework. Internal assessments and assignments are scheduled throughout August and September.

**October — Odd Semester classes conclude.** Study leave is provided before the November examinations. Students are expected to complete all internal assessment requirements before the end of October.

**November — Odd Semester Examinations.** University examinations are conducted across all programmes. Results are typically published within six to eight weeks.

**December — Even Semester begins.** Students register for their next set of courses. Research scholars renew their semester registration and submit progress reports.

**March — Even Semester classes conclude.** A study leave period precedes the April/May examinations.

**April/May — Even Semester Examinations.** University examinations for all programmes. Final-year students complete their dissertation submissions prior to or alongside these examinations.

**May–June — Vacation.** This is the ideal window for summer internships, research projects, language courses, and competitive examination preparation.

## Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)

CUSAT follows the **Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)**, which structures your academic workload around credits rather than just contact hours. Under CBCS, you have the flexibility to choose from:

- **Interdisciplinary courses** — subjects outside your primary department
- **Intra-disciplinary electives** — advanced topics within your own field
- **Skill-based courses** — practical, employment-oriented modules

This system allows you to customise your education, broaden your perspective, and accumulate credits from diverse learning experiences — including online courses from platforms like NPTEL and Swayam.

<Tip>
  Plan your CBCS elective choices early each semester. Popular interdisciplinary and skill-based courses fill up quickly. Consult your faculty advisor at the start of each semester to map out your credit requirements.
</Tip>
