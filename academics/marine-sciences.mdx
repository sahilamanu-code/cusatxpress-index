CUSAT's School of Marine Sciences stands as the **largest marine sciences academic facility in India**, a distinction that reflects decades of investment in research infrastructure, faculty expertise, and fieldwork capabilities. Located on the scenic **Lakeside Campus** along the shores of Vembanad Lake, the marine sciences complex gives you direct access to one of India's richest aquatic ecosystems. If you are drawn to the ocean, the atmosphere, or the life it sustains, CUSAT's marine faculty offers a depth of specialisation found nowhere else in the country.

## School of Marine Sciences

The School of Marine Sciences is the umbrella institution for CUSAT's six marine-focused departments. It coordinates research, postgraduate programmes, and doctoral work across oceanography, marine biology, atmospheric sciences, and geology. The school's Lakeside Campus houses **one of the largest marine science libraries in Asia**, providing access to a vast collection of journals, theses, charts, and datasets.

The school has active research partnerships with the **National Institute of Oceanography (NIO)**, **Indian National Centre for Ocean Information Services (INCOIS)**, **Kerala University of Fisheries and Ocean Studies (KUFOS)**, and several international marine research institutions.

## Marine Science Departments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Department of Atmospheric Sciences" icon="cloud">
    The Department of Atmospheric Sciences offers **M.Sc.** and **PhD** programmes focused on understanding the dynamics of Earth's atmosphere, with particular emphasis on the Indian subcontinent's unique meteorological conditions.

    **Research areas:**
    - Stratosphere–Troposphere Interaction
    - Monsoon Meteorology and Prediction
    - Atmospheric Chemistry
    - Climate Variability and Change

    The department operates advanced meteorological instrumentation and maintains data collaboration with the India Meteorological Department (IMD) and ISRO's Space Applications Centre.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Chemical Oceanography" icon="flask">
    The Department of Chemical Oceanography examines the chemistry of the ocean — how elements cycle through seawater, sediments, and marine life. It offers **M.Sc.** and **PhD** programmes.

    **Research areas:**
    - Trace Metal Distribution in Coastal Waters
    - Marine Bioactive Components and Natural Products
    - Nutrient Dynamics and Eutrophication
    - Organic Geochemistry

    The department's laboratories are equipped for trace-level chemical analysis of seawater and sediment samples, with regular research expeditions along the Kerala and Indian Ocean coastlines.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Physical Oceanography" icon="waves">
    Physical Oceanography at CUSAT focuses on ocean circulation, coastal dynamics, and the application of physical principles to marine systems. The department offers **M.Sc.** and **PhD** programmes.

    **Research areas:**
    - Coastal Zone Management
    - Ocean Modelling and Numerical Simulation
    - Tidal Dynamics and Storm Surge Prediction
    - Estuarine Circulation

    Given CUSAT's location on the southwest coast of India, the department has exceptional access to estuarine, coastal, and open-ocean environments for real-world fieldwork and data collection.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Marine Biology, Microbiology and Biochemistry" icon="fish">
    This department has been at the forefront of marine biological research since **1938**, producing approximately **600 research papers** over its distinguished history. It offers **M.Sc.** and **PhD** programmes.

    **Research areas:**
    - Marine Biodiversity and Ecology
    - Marine Microbiology and Biotechnology
    - Marine Biochemistry and Natural Products
    - Coral Reef and Mangrove Ecology

    Students benefit from the Lakeside Campus location, the university's research vessels, and strong ties to marine protected areas along the Kerala coast.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department of Marine Geology and Geophysics" icon="mountain">
    The Department of Marine Geology and Geophysics investigates the structure, history, and resources of the ocean floor. It offers **M.Sc.** and **PhD** programmes.

    **Research areas:**
    - Marine Mineral Resources (polymetallic nodules, gas hydrates)
    - Seismology and Earthquake Hazard Assessment
    - Sedimentology and Stratigraphy
    - Marine Geomorphology

    The department has contributed substantially to India's understanding of seismic risk along the peninsular coasts and has participated in national ocean surveys conducted by the Geological Survey of India and the Ministry of Earth Sciences.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## School of Industrial Fisheries

The School of Industrial Fisheries is a dedicated institution within CUSAT's marine sciences complex, offering **B.Sc.**, **M.Sc.**, and **PhD** programmes. Fisheries science is uniquely important in Kerala, a state whose economy and culture are deeply intertwined with the sea.

**Research areas include:**
- Fishery Biology and Stock Assessment
- Fish Processing Technology and Quality Control
- Aquaculture Systems
- Fisheries Economics and Management
- Post-Harvest Technology

The school works closely with the Central Marine Fisheries Research Institute (CMFRI), the National Fisheries Development Board, and the Kerala Fisheries Department, giving students unparalleled access to practitioners and policy-makers.

## National Centre for Aquatic Animal Health (NCAAH)

The **National Centre for Aquatic Animal Health (NCAAH)** is a specialised research centre housed within CUSAT's marine sciences complex. It focuses on the health management of aquatic organisms — critical for India's aquaculture industry. The centre conducts diagnostic work, disease surveillance, and capacity building for the aquaculture sector.

## Lakeside Campus

All marine sciences departments are located on the **Lakeside Campus**, situated on the banks of Vembanad Lake in Ernakulam. The campus provides:

- Direct access to backwater and estuarine ecosystems
- Berthing facilities for research boats
- Dedicated marine laboratories and aquaria
- The marine science library
