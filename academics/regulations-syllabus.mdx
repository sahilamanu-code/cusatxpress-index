## Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)

CUSAT implements the **Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)** across its programmes, in alignment with the University Grants Commission's (UGC) national framework. CBCS gives you the flexibility to design a portion of your curriculum according to your interests and career goals.

Under CBCS, your programme includes:

- **Core courses** — mandatory subjects specific to your department
- **Interdisciplinary courses** — subjects from departments outside your faculty
- **Intra-disciplinary electives** — specialised topics within your own field
- **Skill-based courses** — practical modules focused on employability and technical skills

<Info>
  The total credits required for degree completion vary by programme. Refer to the [Academic Regulations](https://cusat.ac.in/student/regulations) page for your specific programme's credit framework.
</Info>

## PhD Regulations

Doctoral candidates at CUSAT are governed by a separate and detailed set of regulations covering admission procedures, coursework requirements, research progress evaluation, thesis submission, and viva voce examination.

You can access the full PhD regulations at:

👉 [cusat.ac.in/student/phd-regulations](https://cusat.ac.in/student/phd-regulations)

Key aspects of the PhD regulations include:
- Eligibility and entrance test requirements
- Coursework credit requirements (typically one semester of structured coursework)
- Six-monthly progress review process
- Pre-submission seminar requirements
- Thesis format and submission guidelines
- External examiner appointment procedures

## Old Regulations (Archive)

If you need to reference regulations that applied to earlier batches — for purposes such as supplementary examination eligibility, arrear clearance, or transcript correction — CUSAT maintains an archive of older regulation documents.

Access archived regulations at:

👉 [dspace.cusat.ac.in](https://dspace.cusat.ac.in) — search the CUSAT digital repository for historical regulation documents by batch year.

<Warning>
  Old regulations apply only to students admitted under those specific batch years. If you are unsure which regulation governs your programme, contact your department office or the university's examination section for clarification.
</Warning>
