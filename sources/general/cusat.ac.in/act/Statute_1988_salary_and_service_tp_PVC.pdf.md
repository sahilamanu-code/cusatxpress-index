Kerala Gazette No.17 dated 26th April 1988. PART I GOVERNMENT OF KERALA Higher Education (J) Department NOTIFICATION G.O.(MS) No.47/88/H.Edn. Dated, Trivandrum, 1st March 1988. S.R.O. No.522/88 – In exercise of the power conferred by sub-section (1) of section 39 of the Cochin University of Science and Technology Act (31 of 1986), read with subsection (3) of section 12 thereof, the Government of Kerala hereby make the following first Statutes governing the salary and other conditions of service of the Pro-Vice-Chancellor of the Cochin University of Science and Technology, namely:- THE COCHIN UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY FIRST STATUES RELATING TO THE SALARY AND OTHER CONDITIONS OF SERVICE OF THE PRO-VICE-CHANCELLOR 1. Short title and commencement – These Statutes may be called the Cochin University of Science and Technology First Statutes relating to the salary and other conditions of service of the Pro-Vice-Chancellor, 1988. (2) They shall be deemed to have come into force on the 19th day of April, 1986. 2. Definition - (1) In these Statutes, unless the context otherwise requires, (a) "Act" means the Cochin University of Science and Technology Act, 1986(31 of 1986); (b) "Pro-Vice-Chancellor" means Pro-Vice-Chancellor of the University, appointed under Section 12. (c) The words and expressions used and not defined to these Statutes, but defined in the Act or in the Interpretation and General Clause Act, 1125, shall have the meaning respectively assigned to them in the said Acts. \*3. Salary and other conditions of service of the Pro-Vice-Chancellor shall be as follows – (1) The Pro-Vice-Chancellor will draw the grade pay and -- 1 of 2 -- Dearness Allowance he would have got, had he continued as Principal, and deputation allowance as prescribed in G.O. (P) 515/85/Fin. dated .the 16th day of September, 1985. (2) He will be paid House Rent Allowance as admissible to University employees provided no quarters is available in the University Campus. (3) He will be permitted the free use of car within 8 Kilometres of the headquarters. (4) He shall be governed by the leave rules of the University. (5) He shall be governed by the Medical attendance rules of the University. (6) In respect of other matters, the provisions of Kerala Service Rules will mutatis-mutandis apply to him. By Order of the Governor, M.S.K. RAMASWAMI, Commissioner & Secretary to Government Explanatory Note (This note does not form part of the notification but is intended to give the general purport). Subsection (3) of section 12 of the Cochin University of Science and Technology Act (31 of 1986) confers powers on the Government to make Statutes prescribing the salary and other conditions of service of the Pro-Vice- Chancellor. Section 39, subsection (1) enjoins that the First Statutes of the University shall be made by the Government, by notification in the Gazette. This notification is intended to lay down the terms and conditions of Service of the Pro-Vice-Chancellor of the Cochin University of Science and Technology. \*Amended -- 2 of 2 --
