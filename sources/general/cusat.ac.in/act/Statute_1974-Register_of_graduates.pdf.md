GOVERNMENT OF KERLA Higher Education (P) Department N O T I F I C A T I O N No.3766/P2/74/H.Edn Dated, Trivandrum, 8th February, 1974. In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 31 of the Cochin University Act, 1971 (30 of 1971) the Government of Kerala hereby make the First Statutes on Register of Graduates of the Cochin University namely:- COCHIN UNIVERSITY STATUTES REGISTER OF GRADUATES 1. Eligibility for registration –(1) The Syndicate shall maintain a register of Registered Graduates in which the Graduate of any of the following classes may have his name entered and retained until he continues to be employed in or normally resident within the territorial jurisdiction of the University. (a) A Graduate of the University of Cochin who became eligible for a Degree in any Faculty not less than three years before the date application for registration and who has taken the degree. (b) A Graduate of any other Statutory University in India or of a Foreign statutory University that may be recognized by the Syndicate for the purpose of registration, domiciled or ordinarily a resident or employed within the territorial jurisdiction or the University who became eligible for a degree not less than three yeas before the date of application for registration and who has taken he degree. (c) Holders of the Honorary Degree of the University of Cochin. (2) The Vice-Chancellor is the competent authority to admit a graduate as the Registered Graduate of the University. 2. Registered Graduates of other Universities in the State: (1) All Registered Graduates of any other University in the State who, on the date of commencement of the Cochin University Act, 1971 are employed in or resident within the territorial limits of the University of Cochin may apply to the University of Cochin to get their names registered as Registered Graduates of the University of Cochin. (2) In the case of persons coming under clause (1), no registration fee need be remitted along with the application for registration but they shall produce the proof of their registration in the other University along with the application for registration. (3) Upon such registration being granted, they shall cease to be registered Graduates of the other University and shall be deemed to be Registered Graduates of the University of Cochin. -- 1 of 3 -- 2 \*3. Fee for Registration: The fee for registration shall be Rs.10 (Rupees ten only), which shall be paid along with the application for registration in the form prescribed by the Vice-Chancellor for the purpose. The Degree Certificate in original shall also be forwarded along with the application for registration. 4. Revision and correction of register - The Register of graduates shall be revised and corrected on the first day of November of each year and applications for revision or correction of the Register of Graduates shall reach the Registrar not less than thirty clelar days before the 1 st day of November each year. 5. Eligibility to participate in elections - A graduate who gets himself registered not less than thirty clear days before the date of publication of electoral roll shall also be eligible to participate in the election. 6. Inspection of Register – Any Graduate whose name is in the register shall be entitled to inspect the register during office hours on application to the Registrar and shall be entitled on payment Rs.10 to have a copy of the list of Registered Graduates corrected up to the last revision under Statute 4. (2) The University reserves to itself the right of printing and reprinting the list of Registered Graduates and supplemental list of Registered Graduates shall be supplied on payment of the price prescribed by the Vice-Chancellor from time to time. 7. Removal of names: -(1) When any communication is posted to a Registered Graduate in the address shown in the register of Registered Graduates and is returned through ‘The dead Letter Office” to the Registrar undelivered with an endorsement by the postal department that the person is reported dead, the name of such Graduate shall be removed from the list. (2) If the Vice- Chancellor, on enquiry, is satisfied that a Graduate on the list is deceased, he shall remove the name of such deceased Graduate from the list. (3) If at any time, after registration as a Registered graduate, any person for any reason is deprived in any manner of his Degree or Degrees which entitled to him to registration as such he shall cease to be a Registered Graduate of the University from the date. \*Amended (4) If the Vice – Chancellor on enquiry is satisfied that a Graduate in the list is Registered Graduate of any other Statutory University in India, he shall remove the name of such person from the Register of Registered Graduates. (5) The Senate may, on the recommendation of the Syndicate, remove the name of any person from the Register of Graduates if he has been convicted by a Court of law for an offence involving moral turpitude.
