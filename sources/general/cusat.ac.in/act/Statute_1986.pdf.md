Dated, Trivandrum, 7th March, 1986. S.R.O. No. 411/86 – In exercise of the powers conferred by section 34 of the Cochin University of Science and Technology Ordinance, 1986 (2 of 1986), read with clause (1) of Section 33 thereof, the Government of Kerala hereby make the following First Statutes for the Constitution of the First Syndicate and Academic Council of the University, namely:- 1. Short title and commencement – (1) These Statutes may be called the Cochin University of Science and Technology First Statutes for the first constitution of the Syndicate and the Academic Council. (2) They shall come into force at once. 2. Definitions – (1) In these Statutes, unless the context otherwise requires- (a) "Ordinance" means the Cochin University of Science and Technology Ordinance, 1986 (2 of 1986): (b) The words and expressions used and not defined in these Statutes, but defined in the Ordinance, or in the Interpretation and General Clauses Act, 1125 shall have the meaning respectively assigned to them in that Ordinance and that Act. -- 1 of 2 -- 2 3. First Constitution of the Syndicate and Academic Council of the University – (1) Subject to the provisions of the Ordinance, the Chancellor shall constitute the first Syndicate and first Academic Council of the University. (2) Constitution of Syndicate and Academic Council to be notified – The Constitution of the Syndicate and Academic Council of the University shall be notified in the Gazette. By Order of the Governor, M.DANDAPANI Commissioner and Secretary Explanatory Note (This does not form part of the notification, but is intended to give its general purport). Section 34 of the Cochin University of Science and Technology Ordinance, 1986 (2 of 1986) confers power on the Government to make the first Statutes of the Cochin University of Science and Technology. It is considered necessary to frame the first Statutes enabling the constitution of the Syndicate and the Academic Council immediately. The Notification is intended to achieve the object. -- 2 of 2 --
