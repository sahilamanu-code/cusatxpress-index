### SDG 14 Life Below Water

The School of Marine Science at Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT) is a distinguished institution, established in 1938, that stands as a pioneer in ocean research and education in India. This remarkable center is dedicated to the vital mission of conserving and sustainably utilizing the world's oceans, seas, and marine resources. Within its expansive framework, the School encompasses several specialized departments: the Department of Atmospheric Sciences, which explores the interactions between the atmosphere and ocean; the Department of Chemical Oceanography, focusing on the chemical processes in marine environments; the Department of Physical Oceanography, examining ocean dynamics and circulation; the Department of Marine Biology, Microbiology and Biochemistry, investigating marine organisms and their biochemical processes; and the Department of Marine Geology, which studies the geological aspects of marine environments. Additionally, the National Centre for Aquatic Animal Health plays a crucial role in ensuring the well-being of aquatic species, while the School of Industrial Fisheries addresses the practical aspects of marine resource utilization. CUSAT is at the cutting edge of tackling challenges that affect oceanic and coastal regions. Through a combination of innovative courses and comprehensive research and development projects, the institution is committed to evolving scientific solutions that address environmental issues, promote sustainability, and foster a deeper understanding of marine ecosystems.
