## ACADEMIC COUNCIL

People who work in the University's Administration and Services are essential for supporting the mission of CUSAT. These employees deal with everything from the University's human and financial resources to health and safety matters. Syndicate, Academic Council, Senate and the Boards of Studies in different faculties are the Principal Authorities of the university. Syndicate is the Chief Executive Body which meets every month.
