Interacting with the faculty and students at the Polymer Science Auditorium of the Cochin University of Science and Technology, Prof.Alferov said that instru mentation for measurement, characterisation, materials prepara- tion and device fabrication has be- come an important area. Earlier, the instruments needed for research were made in the laboratory itself. Now, people depend more on commercial instruments, he said. Emphasing the role of mathematics in science, Prof.Alferov said he had learnt math- ematical modelling and its use in his area from the legendary Prof.Peter Kapitza. This knowledge helped him a lot in his research, he said. Urging the students to find out their area of specialization according to one’s ability and interests, Prof.Alferov said he would like to go back and become a student, given an opportunity.” I would take up Physics and spend more time in the laboratory”, he said. Answering a question whether he had expected to win the Nobel Prize, Prof.Alferov said he knew the impor- tance of his discoveries and hence he expected it.He said he used to spend long hours in the laboratory during his days of research, practically living in the laboratory on several occa- sions. Prof. Alferov said the credit for his Nobel Prize goes to his parents, and his brother who died in the battlefield during the Second World War. He also recalled the contribu- tions made by his teachers in shaping his career in science. Expressing his difficulty in using English, Prof.Alferov said he had his education in the Russian language. Pointing out that he had felt the need to learn English, he said it was required while interact- ing with the global scientific commu- nity.
