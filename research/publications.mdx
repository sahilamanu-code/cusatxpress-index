CUSAT's research community produces a steady and growing volume of scholarship published in peer-reviewed national and international journals, conference proceedings, and book chapters. The university maintains a centralised publications database at [cusat.ac.in/publications](https://cusat.ac.in/publications) where you can browse and search the full record of faculty and scholar research output from 2020 through the present. Whether you are a prospective student evaluating research strengths, a collaborator identifying potential partners, or a researcher auditing departmental output, this portal provides a comprehensive starting point.

## About the Publications Portal

The publications database at [cusat.ac.in/publications](https://cusat.ac.in/publications) is maintained by the Research and Development Cell and updated regularly as new papers are published and reported by faculty.

<Note>
  The portal is searchable by **year**, **author name**, and **department**. When citing publications sourced through the portal, always verify the final published version through the journal's official website or DOI link. For formal citations, use the standard format of the target journal or conference (APA, MLA, IEEE, Vancouver, etc.) and include the DOI wherever available. CUSAT affiliation should be cited as: *[Department Name], Cochin University of Science and Technology, Kochi 682022, Kerala, India.*
</Note>

## Breadth of Disciplines Represented

Publications in the database span CUSAT's full range of academic disciplines, including but not limited to:

- **Marine Sciences** — oceanography, coastal ecology, marine biodiversity, fisheries
- **Computer Science and AI** — machine learning, natural language processing, cryptography, distributed systems
- **Physics and Materials Science** — nanostructures, thin films, laser physics, condensed matter
- **Chemistry** — electrochemical sensors, photocatalysis, polymer chemistry, green synthesis
- **Environmental Sciences** — pollution assessment, forest ecology, aquatic toxicology, climate impact
- **Polymer Science** — biomaterials, nanocomposites, energy harvesting materials
- **Management and Social Sciences** — entrepreneurship, HRM, fiscal policy, maritime law

## Recent Research Highlights

The following examples illustrate the diversity and quality of research being published by CUSAT scholars and faculty in recent years:

<Accordion title="Computer Science & AI">
  Recent publications from the Computer Science and Computer Applications departments span:

  - **Semantic Text Kernels** — novel kernel methods for natural language understanding tasks
  - **Drug Synergy Prediction** — machine learning models for predicting combination therapy outcomes in oncology
  - **Machine Unlearning** — emerging research on selectively removing data from trained neural networks to address privacy and compliance requirements

  These papers appear in journals such as *Expert Systems with Applications*, *Computers in Biology and Medicine*, and *IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems*.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Physics & Biomedical Sciences">
  The Physics department and its interdisciplinary collaborators have published on:

  - **Clustering in Hot Si-28** — nuclear structure studies exploring cluster configurations in excited silicon nuclei
  - **Gut–Brain Axis** — molecular investigations of the bidirectional communication between the gastrointestinal system and the central nervous system, published in collaboration with the Biotechnology department

  These contributions appear in journals such as *Physical Review C*, *Nuclear Physics A*, and *Frontiers in Neuroscience*.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Environmental & Atmospheric Sciences">
  Environmental and atmospheric researchers have published work including:

  - **Forest Fires and Aerosol Loading** — studies using remote sensing data to characterise aerosol optical depth and its climatic effects during forest fire events in South Asia
  - **Coastal Water Quality** — monitoring and modelling studies of estuarine and nearshore systems in Kerala

  Papers appear in *Atmospheric Environment*, *Science of the Total Environment*, and *Journal of Geophysical Research: Atmospheres*.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Marine Sciences">
  The School of Marine Sciences continues to produce high-volume output across subdisciplines:

  - Marine biodiversity surveys and species descriptions
  - Trace metal geochemistry in estuarine systems
  - Climate change impacts on coral reefs and seagrass beds
  - Mangrove carbon stock assessments

  Key publication venues include *Marine Pollution Bulletin*, *Estuarine, Coastal and Shelf Science*, *Journal of the Marine Biological Association of the UK*, and *Chemosphere*.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Polymer Science & Rubber Technology">
  With **700+ cumulative publications**, the Polymer Science & Rubber Technology department regularly publishes in:

  - *Polymer* — on nanocomposite synthesis and characterisation
  - *Journal of Applied Polymer Science* — on processing and mechanical properties
  - *ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces* — on energy harvesting and functional biomaterials

  The department's publication record represents one of the largest and most sustained outputs of any CUSAT department.
</Accordion>

## Accessing the Database

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Publications Portal">
    Go to [cusat.ac.in/publications](https://cusat.ac.in/publications) to access the full database.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search by Author, Year, or Department">
    Use the available filters to narrow results to a specific faculty member, time period, or academic department.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the DOI or Journal Link">
    Each listing includes a link to the published article. Use this to access the full text via your institutional or personal journal subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact the RDC for Corrections">
    If you are a CUSAT faculty member and notice a missing or incorrect entry, contact the Research and Development Cell through [cusat.ac.in/rdc](https://cusat.ac.in/rdc) to request an update.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  PhD scholars who have published journal articles as part of their doctoral work should ensure their publications are reported to their department and the RDC so they can be included in the database. This record contributes to CUSAT's institutional research profile and national ranking assessments.
</Info>
