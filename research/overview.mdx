CUSAT has built one of India's most dynamic university research ecosystems, spanning fundamental sciences, applied engineering, marine studies, social sciences, and beyond. At the heart of this effort is the **Research and Development Cell (RDC)**, which fosters a strong research culture aimed at contributing to a self-reliant India. Through its recognised departments, external research centres, and industry linkages, CUSAT ensures that knowledge creation translates into real-world innovation and societal benefit.

## Research and Development Cell (RDC)

The RDC coordinates CUSAT's research strategy and infrastructure. Its mandate is to establish a vibrant research ecosystem that produces reliable, impactful, and sustainable outcomes — supporting everything from early-stage doctoral work to large-scale collaborative projects with industry and government.

Key responsibilities of the RDC include:

- Facilitating PhD registrations across departments and the 26 recognised external research centres
- Coordinating research funding applications to national agencies
- Promoting international academic and industry collaborations
- Supporting technology transfer and intellectual property (IP) management

Visit [cusat.ac.in/rdc](https://cusat.ac.in/rdc) to learn more or contact the RDC directly.

## Scale and Output

CUSAT's research output reflects decades of sustained investment and faculty expertise:

- The **Department of Physics** alone has produced approximately **150 PhDs** and contributed over **1,200 publications** in international journals.
- The **Department of Polymer Science & Rubber Technology** (established 1971) has accumulated **700+ publications** and **15+ patents** filed.
- The **Department of Marine Biology, Microbiology & Biochemistry** has published **~600 research papers** across marine and environmental sciences.
- The **Department of Biotechnology** (established 1991) has guided approximately **75 PhD scholars**.

Across all departments, CUSAT researchers publish regularly in high-impact national and international journals. You can explore the full publications database at [cusat.ac.in/publications](https://cusat.ac.in/publications).

## Funding Partners

CUSAT research is supported by a wide range of national and international funding bodies:

- **UGC** (University Grants Commission)
- **DST** (Department of Science and Technology)
- **DBT** (Department of Biotechnology)
- **MHRD** (Ministry of Human Resource Development)
- **CSIR** (Council of Scientific and Industrial Research)
- International research partners and bilateral collaboration schemes

<Info>
  The Department of Atmospheric Sciences has been identified as a **Centre of Excellence** by CSIR under the Government of India's NMITLI (New Millennium Indian Technology Leadership Initiative) scheme.
</Info>

## International Collaborations

CUSAT maintains active research collaborations with leading universities and institutions worldwide:

- **Applied Chemistry** — NTU Singapore, University of Limerick (Ireland), Polish Academy of Sciences
- **Photonics (ISP)** — Eindhoven University of Technology (Netherlands), NTU Singapore
- **Physics** — Iwate University (Japan), TU Eindhoven (Netherlands), Penn State University (USA)
- **Ship Technology** — University of Rostock (Germany), collaboration active since 1978
- **Statistics** — University of Waterloo and Dalhousie University (Canada), University of Warwick (UK)

These partnerships enable joint publications, faculty and student exchange, and co-supervised doctoral research.

## Make in India — ACARR

The **Advanced Centre for Atmospheric Radar Research (ACARR)** at CUSAT is a flagship initiative under the Government of India's **Make in India** programme. ACARR develops indigenous atmospheric radar systems, contributing to national capability in weather monitoring and atmospheric science research.
