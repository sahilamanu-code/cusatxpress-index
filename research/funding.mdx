Securing research funding is a critical step in building a successful academic career, and CUSAT's researchers benefit from access to a wide range of national and international grant schemes. Whether you are a faculty member leading a sponsored project or a doctoral scholar looking for fellowship support, the funding landscape at CUSAT is robust. The university's Research and Development Cell (RDC) actively supports grant applications and helps researchers identify the right funding sources for their work.

For a regularly updated list of open calls, visit [cusat.ac.in/funding](https://cusat.ac.in/funding).

## National Funding Agencies

The following central government agencies fund a wide range of research projects at CUSAT:

<Accordion title="UGC — University Grants Commission">
  The **University Grants Commission** funds individual and collaborative research through multiple schemes:

  - **Major Research Projects (MRP)** — multi-year grants for faculty-led research
  - **Minor Research Projects** — smaller grants for exploratory and pilot studies
  - **Emeritus Fellowships** — support for retired but active faculty
  - **Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) / Senior Research Fellowship (SRF)** — for NET-qualified doctoral scholars
  - **Dr. D.S. Kothari Post-Doctoral Fellowships** — for early-career researchers

  UGC schemes are open to all departments. Check [ugc.ac.in](https://ugc.ac.in) for current calls.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="DST — Department of Science and Technology">
  **DST** operates several competitive funding programmes relevant to CUSAT researchers:

  - **SERB (Science and Engineering Research Board)** — Core Research Grants (CRG), MATRICS, CRG, and TARE schemes for individual faculty
  - **INSPIRE** — Innovation in Science Pursuit for Inspired Research: fellowships for doctoral and post-doctoral researchers
  - **FIST (Fund for Improvement of S&T Infrastructure)** — institutional grants for upgrading laboratory infrastructure
  - **WOS-A / WOS-B** — Women Scientists Schemes for career re-entry
  - **NIDHI (National Initiative for Developing and Harnessing Innovations)** — supports startups and technology ventures

  Visit [dst.gov.in](https://dst.gov.in) to explore current DST opportunities.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="DBT — Department of Biotechnology">
  **DBT** funds cutting-edge life sciences and biotechnology research, particularly relevant to CUSAT's Biotechnology, Marine Biology, and Environmental Studies departments:

  - **DBT Research Grants** — for individual PIs and collaborative consortia
  - **BINC (Bioinformatics National Certification)** — scholarships and fellowships for bioinformatics
  - **DBT-JRF / SRF** — junior and senior research fellowships for DBT-NET qualified scholars
  - **BioCARe** — career development awards for women scientists in biotechnology
  - **BUILDER** — for building institutional research capacity

  Visit [dbtindia.gov.in](https://dbtindia.gov.in) for scheme details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="CSIR — Council of Scientific and Industrial Research">
  **CSIR** supports applied and translational research through:

  - **CSIR-JRF / SRF** — fellowships for NET-qualified researchers in science and engineering
  - **Extramural Research (EMR)** — grants for CSIR network labs and academic institutions
  - **NMITLI (New Millennium Indian Technology Leadership Initiative)** — large consortium grants for mission-mode research

  CUSAT's Department of Atmospheric Sciences holds CSIR Centre of Excellence status under NMITLI, making CUSAT an experienced institution for CSIR-funded work.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="MHRD / MoE and RUSA">
  The **Ministry of Education (formerly MHRD)** and its programmes fund infrastructure and research capacity:

  - **RUSA (Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan)** — provides project-based **RUSA Internships** and research grants to eligible state universities including CUSAT
  - **IMPRINT India** — interdisciplinary research for India's development needs
  - **SPARC (Scheme for Promotion of Academic and Research Collaboration)** — joint research projects between Indian and foreign universities, supporting faculty and student exchange

  RUSA internships are an excellent avenue for doctoral scholars to engage in funded project work while pursuing their degree.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="ICMR — Indian Council of Medical Research">
  **ICMR** supports biomedical and health sciences research:

  - **ICMR-JRF** — fellowships for medical and health sciences doctoral candidates
  - **Extramural Research Grants** — for individual faculty in health-related disciplines
  - **Short-term Studentships (STS)** — for undergraduate and postgraduate students to gain research exposure

  ICMR funding is particularly relevant for CUSAT researchers in Biotechnology, Marine Biology, and related biomedical fields.
</Accordion>

## International Funding Opportunities

<Accordion title="SPARC — Promotion of Academic and Research Collaboration">
  **SPARC** (under MoE) funds collaborative research projects between Indian institutions and top-ranked foreign universities. Under SPARC, CUSAT researchers can:

  - Co-author publications and jointly mentor PhD students with foreign partners
  - Access research infrastructure at partner universities
  - Invite foreign faculty to CUSAT for short-term teaching and research visits

  CUSAT already has active MoUs with universities in Singapore, Netherlands, Germany, Japan, USA, Canada, Ireland, and the UK — many of which qualify for SPARC collaboration.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="DST-CETP — Clean Energy Transition Partnership">
  The **DST-CETP** scheme supports India's clean energy research agenda through bilateral and multilateral international funding. CUSAT researchers in Polymer Science (energy harvesting), Physics (solar materials), and Environmental Studies are well-positioned to benefit from this scheme.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Other International Sources">
  CUSAT researchers have also accessed funding from:

  - **European Research Council (ERC)** collaboration grants via institutional partners
  - **Newton-Bhabha Fund** — UK–India research partnerships
  - **DAAD (German Academic Exchange Service)** — for India-Germany research collaboration (relevant for Ship Technology and Physics)
  - **Indo-US Science and Technology Forum (IUSSTF)** — joint research workshops and fellowships
</Accordion>

## Students Aid Fund

CUSAT invites applications for the **Students Aid Fund** annually to provide financial support to scholars facing economic hardship during their academic and research programmes. This fund is separate from research project grants and is intended to help students sustain their studies without financial disruption.

<Tip>
  Monitor [cusat.ac.in/news](https://cusat.ac.in/news) for annual Students Aid Fund notifications and other financial assistance schemes. Application windows are typically announced at the beginning of each academic year.
</Tip>

## Finding and Applying for Funding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the Funding Portal">
    Start at [cusat.ac.in/funding](https://cusat.ac.in/funding) for an updated list of active funding opportunities relevant to CUSAT researchers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify the Right Scheme">
    Match your research area, career stage (faculty / post-doc / doctoral scholar), and project type (individual / collaborative / infrastructure) to the appropriate agency and scheme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact the RDC">
    The Research and Development Cell provides guidance on application procedures, institutional endorsement, and project account management. Visit [cusat.ac.in/rdc](https://cusat.ac.in/rdc).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Through Official Portals">
    Most national agencies use online submission portals (e.g., SERB's ANRF portal, DST online, DBT e-grants). Ensure you have your institutional details, ethics clearances (if applicable), and co-investigator confirmations ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Check both [cusat.ac.in/funding](https://cusat.ac.in/funding) and [cusat.ac.in/news](https://cusat.ac.in/news) regularly — new grant calls, fellowship deadlines, and funding announcements are posted as they are received by the university.
</Tip>
