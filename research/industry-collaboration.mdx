CUSAT has long recognised that the full value of university research is realised only when it reaches industry, society, and the marketplace. To bridge the gap between academic innovation and real-world application, CUSAT operates a rich network of industry engagement platforms — from a student-focused entrepreneurship incubator to national-level radar research and formalised consultancy services offered by individual departments. Whether you are a doctoral scholar looking to connect your research to industry, a startup founder seeking incubation support, or a company seeking specialised testing and advisory services, CUSAT has structured pathways for engagement.

## CITTIC — Centre for Innovation Technology Transfer and Industrial Collaboration

**CITTIC** is CUSAT's primary hub for student entrepreneurship and technology transfer. Operating at the intersection of academia and industry, CITTIC provides structured support to help researchers and students convert ideas into viable ventures.

- **Website:** [cittic.cusat.ac.in](https://cittic.cusat.ac.in)
- **Focus:** Student and faculty entrepreneurship, early-stage startup support, and technology transfer facilitation

### What CITTIC Offers

- **Startup Incubation** — mentoring, workspace, and early-stage support for technology-based startups founded by CUSAT students and alumni
- **Innovation Challenges** — inter-departmental hackathons, ideathons, and product design competitions
- **Industry Linkages** — connecting students with industry mentors, internship opportunities, and collaborative research projects
- **Technology Transfer** — facilitating the transfer of CUSAT research outputs to industry through licensing and spin-off creation
- **Skill Development** — workshops on design thinking, prototyping, business model development, and funding readiness

<Info>
  If you have a startup idea or an early prototype developed during your time at CUSAT, CITTIC is your first point of contact. You do not need a complete product to approach them — they support ventures at the ideation stage and beyond.
</Info>

## CUSATECH Foundation

The **CUSATECH Foundation** is a **Section 8 company** (not-for-profit under the Companies Act) established at CUSAT to serve as its **Technology Business Incubator (TBI)** and **Employability & Innovation Hub**.

As the formal TBI at CUSAT, CUSATECH Foundation:

- Provides incubation infrastructure and mentoring to deep-tech and applied-science startups
- Facilitates the commercialisation of CUSAT's patent portfolio and intellectual property
- Connects faculty inventors with industry partners, investors, and licensing opportunities
- Supports employability initiatives by building industry-relevant skills among students and scholars
- Works alongside the **Inter University Centre for IPR Studies** to ensure IP is protected before commercialisation

CUSATECH is the institutional bridge between CUSAT's research outputs and the commercial world.

## DESCON — Ship Technology Design Consultancy

The **Department of Ship Technology** operates **DESCON** ([descon.cusat.ac.in](https://descon.cusat.ac.in)), a specialised design consultancy providing professional services to the maritime industry. DESCON offers:

- **Ship Design and Naval Architecture** — hull form development, stability analysis, and structural design
- **Marine Hydrodynamics Consultancy** — CFD-based drag and resistance optimisation
- **Corrosion Engineering Advisory** — anti-corrosion coating specifications and cathodic protection design
- **Structural Surveys and Assessments** — independent evaluation of marine and offshore structures

DESCON draws on the department's long-standing collaboration with the **University of Rostock** (Germany) — active since 1978 — and its well-equipped hydrodynamics and structural testing laboratories.

## ACARR — Advanced Centre for Atmospheric Radar Research

The **Advanced Centre for Atmospheric Radar Research (ACARR)** at CUSAT is a flagship national initiative developed under the Government of India's **Make in India** programme. ACARR focuses on the indigenous design, development, and deployment of atmospheric radar systems for weather monitoring, climate research, and disaster preparedness.

As part of Make in India, ACARR contributes to:

- Building domestic capability in atmospheric radar manufacturing, reducing import dependence
- Advancing research in monsoon dynamics, severe weather prediction, and atmospheric boundary layer studies
- Providing weather and atmospheric data in support of national agencies and disaster management authorities

ACARR represents the intersection of CUSAT's atmospheric science expertise and government-backed national manufacturing priorities.

## MoUs with Industry and International Universities

CUSAT has signed **Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs)** with a broad range of industry partners and international universities. These agreements facilitate:

- **Joint Research Projects** — co-funded studies with shared IP arrangements
- **Student and Faculty Exchange** — semester visits, joint supervision, and research stays
- **Collaborative Degree Programmes** — joint and dual-degree arrangements at postgraduate and doctoral level
- **Industry Internships** — structured placements for CUSAT students in partner companies

Active international university partnerships include institutions in Germany (Rostock), Netherlands (Eindhoven), Japan (Iwate), USA (Penn State), Singapore (NTU), Ireland (Limerick), Canada (Waterloo, Dalhousie), Poland, and the UK (Warwick).

## Testing, Analysis, and Training Services

CUSAT also provides **testing, analysis, and training services** to external organisations through its specialised centres:

- **STIC (Sophisticated Test & Instrumentation Centre)** — one of CUSAT's most widely used service units, offering analytical instrumentation services including XRD, SEM, TEM, NMR, and spectroscopy to external academic institutions, research labs, and industries
- **Departmental labs** in Physics, Chemistry, Polymer Science, and Instrumentation accept external samples for characterisation and testing
- **Short-term training programmes** — departments offer certificate and short-term courses for industry professionals in areas such as instrumentation, ocean data analysis, polymer testing, and statistical methods
