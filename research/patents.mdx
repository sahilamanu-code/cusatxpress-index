Innovation at CUSAT does not stop at publication — it extends into protecting and commercialising original inventions through patents and other forms of intellectual property (IP). The university actively encourages faculty, researchers, and students to file patent applications when their research yields novel, protectable inventions. CUSAT's patent activities are supported by a dedicated IPR facilitation centre and a technology commercialisation arm, ensuring that inventors receive the guidance they need throughout the patent lifecycle.

You can explore CUSAT's full patent portfolio at [cusat.ac.in/research-patents](https://cusat.ac.in/research-patents).

## CUSAT Patent Portfolio

CUSAT holds patents across a range of technical disciplines. The depth of patent activity reflects the applied orientation of research in several key departments:

- **Polymer Science & Rubber Technology** — the department with the most extensive IP record at CUSAT, with **15+ patents filed** covering nanomaterials, biomaterials, energy harvesting composites, and specialty rubber formulations.
- **Applied Chemistry** — patents relating to electrochemical sensor designs and photocatalytic materials.
- **Instrumentation** — patents on embedded sensing systems and nano-particle embedded polymer films.
- **Photonics (ISP)** — patents relating to fiber optic sensor systems and laser-based devices.
- **Biotechnology** — patents on biological processes, plant-derived compounds, and biomedical applications.
- **Ship Technology** — patents on corrosion-resistant coatings and marine structural designs.

## Inter University Centre for IPR Studies

The **Inter University Centre for Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) Studies** is housed within the **School of Legal Studies** at CUSAT. It is one of the few dedicated IPR academic and facilitation centres in the Indian university system, offering both teaching programmes and practical IPR support services to the CUSAT research community and beyond.

### Services Offered

- **Patent Filing Assistance** — guidance through the process of filing patent applications with the Indian Patent Office (IPO), including prior art searches, claim drafting support, and filing coordination
- **IP Counselling** — advising researchers on the patentability of their inventions and the most appropriate form of IP protection (patent, copyright, trade secret, design right)
- **Licensing Guidance** — assistance with licensing agreements and technology transfer contracts
- **IP Awareness Workshops** — regular training sessions for faculty, research scholars, and students on IP fundamentals, the patent system, and commercialisation strategies
- **Trademark and Copyright Advice** — support for departments and units seeking to protect institutional branding or creative works

## Commercialising CUSAT Inventions

Holding a patent is only valuable if the underlying technology reaches the market or solves a real-world problem. CUSAT supports technology commercialisation through:

### CUSATECH Foundation

The **CUSATECH Foundation** is a Section 8 (not-for-profit) company that serves as CUSAT's **Technology Business Incubator** and **Employability & Innovation Hub**. Among its key roles:

- Facilitating the licensing of CUSAT-owned patents to industry partners
- Supporting faculty and student inventors in forming technology-based startups
- Connecting IP owners with venture capital, angel investors, and industry buyers
- Managing technology transfer agreements between CUSAT and external organisations

### CITTIC

The **Centre for Innovation Technology Transfer and Industrial Collaboration (CITTIC)** at [cittic.cusat.ac.in](https://cittic.cusat.ac.in) also plays a supporting role in connecting CUSAT's IP with potential industry collaborators and startup ventures, particularly through student entrepreneurship programmes.

## Filing a Patent — Key Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify a Patentable Invention">
    A patentable invention must be novel, involve an inventive step (non-obvious), and be capable of industrial application. If your research has produced something new and useful, it may qualify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact the IPR Centre Before Publishing">
    Reach out to the **Inter University Centre for IPR Studies** before submitting your work for publication or presenting it at a conference. Public disclosure before filing can jeopardise patent rights.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conduct a Prior Art Search">
    The IPR Centre will assist you in searching existing patents and literature to confirm novelty and refine the scope of your claims.
  </Step>
  <Step title="File the Application">
    Work with the IPR Centre to prepare and file a provisional or complete patent application with the Indian Patent Office. For international protection, discuss PCT (Patent Cooperation Treaty) filing options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track and Maintain">
    Monitor the status of your application through the IPO portal and ensure renewal fees are paid on granted patents. The IPR Centre can assist with this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commercialise Through CUSATECH">
    Once your patent is filed or granted, engage CUSATECH Foundation to explore licensing, startup formation, or industry transfer opportunities.
  </Step>
</Steps>
