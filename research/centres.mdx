Beyond its own campus departments, CUSAT has established a network of **26 recognised external research centres** spread across Kerala and beyond. These are premier national laboratories, government research institutes, and specialised centres where CUSAT-registered PhD scholars can conduct their doctoral research under approved supervisors. This arrangement extends CUSAT's research footprint into some of India's finest scientific facilities and ensures doctoral candidates have access to world-class infrastructure and expertise.

If you are a prospective or current PhD student, you may be eligible to register through one of these centres rather than — or in addition to — a CUSAT campus department. For eligibility criteria and registration procedures, contact the Research and Development Cell at [cusat.ac.in/rdc](https://cusat.ac.in/rdc).

## Why Pursue Research at an External Centre?

Conducting your doctoral research at a recognised external centre offers several advantages:

- Access to specialised infrastructure, equipment, and datasets not available on campus
- Exposure to mission-driven research environments (e.g., ISRO facilities, CSIR labs)
- Co-supervision arrangements bridging campus faculty expertise with centre scientists
- Networking with industry and government scientists during your PhD

<Note>
  PhD scholars registered at external research centres remain enrolled at CUSAT and receive a CUSAT degree upon successful completion. All academic requirements, coursework, and thesis submission norms follow CUSAT's PhD regulations.
</Note>
