Once the Examination Wing completes the valuation of answer scripts and tabulates the marks, semester results are published on the official exam portal. You can check your results online at any time after they are announced, view subject-wise marks, and also explore year-wise result statistics and department toppers — all from a single portal.

## How Results Are Processed

The journey from your answer script to your published result involves several steps within the Examination Wing:

1. **Answer scripts are collected** from all exam centres after each paper.
2. **Scripts are forwarded for valuation** to approved examiners.
3. **Marks are received** from examiners and entered into the system.
4. **Tabulation** is carried out to compile subject-wise and aggregate marks.
5. **Results are published** on the official exam portal for students to access.

This process ensures accuracy and fairness before any result goes live.

## Checking Your Results

Results for all programmes are published on the official exam portal as soon as they are finalised. You can access them through either of the two portals:

- **[exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in)** → Navigate to the **Results** section.
- **[estudents.cusat.ac.in](https://estudents.cusat.ac.in)** → Log in and view your results under your student profile.

<Note>
Result notifications and announcements are also posted at [cusat.ac.in/news](https://cusat.ac.in/news). Bookmark this page so you are notified as soon as results are declared for your semester.
</Note>

## Year-Wise Result Status

The Examination Wing publishes a **Year Wise Result Status** on [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) that gives you a programme-level view of how results have been declared across academic years. This is useful if you are tracking pending results from previous semesters or need a historical record of result publication for your batch.

## List of Toppers

CUSAT recognises academic excellence by publishing a **List of Toppers** department-wise on the exam portal. If you are among the top performers in your programme for a given examination, your name will appear in this listing. You can view toppers from [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) under the **List of Toppers** section.

## Re-valuation and Challenge Valuation

If you believe your answer script has not been valued correctly, you can apply for re-valuation or challenge valuation by following the official procedures set out by the Examination Wing.

<Info>
The process, timeline, and fees for re-valuation and challenge valuation are governed by the official orders and circulars published by the Examination Wing. Check the **Orders/Circulars** section on [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) for the currently applicable procedure before submitting any application.
</Info>

Typically, the process involves:

- Submitting a formal application within the prescribed time window after results are declared.
- Paying the applicable re-valuation fee as notified by the Examination Wing.
- Awaiting the revised result, which is published after the re-valuation is complete.

<Warning>
Re-valuation requests submitted after the deadline will not be accepted. Monitor the **Notifications** section on [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) immediately after your results are published to catch the application window in time.
</Warning>

## Common Questions

<Accordion title="How long after exams are results published?">
The time between examinations and results varies by programme and the volume of scripts to be valued. Keep an eye on [cusat.ac.in/news](https://cusat.ac.in/news) and the **Notifications** section on [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) for result announcements.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I get a printed copy of my marks?">
Yes. Once results are published, you can apply for an official **Mark Certificate / Marklist** through the Online Application for Certificates on [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in). See the [Certificates & Forms](/examinations/certificates-forms) page for details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if my result shows an error?">
Contact the Examination Wing directly at **0484-2862240** or **0484-2577109** with your register number and details of the discrepancy. The Wing can guide you on the appropriate correction procedure.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I find results from previous semesters?">
Past semester results remain accessible on [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) under the **Results** section. You can also view your complete result history by logging in to [estudents.cusat.ac.in](https://estudents.cusat.ac.in).
</Accordion>
