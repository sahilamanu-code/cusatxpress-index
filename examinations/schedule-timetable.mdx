Before every examination season, the CUSAT Examination Wing publishes the official timetable and releases hall tickets so you can plan your preparation with complete clarity. Knowing exactly when each exam is scheduled — and having your hall ticket in hand — means you can walk into the exam hall with confidence and without last-minute stress.

## Exam Windows Every Semester

CUSAT runs two examination windows each academic year, one for each semester. Exam dates are fixed so they fall after the teaching period ends, ensuring you get your full classroom instruction before sitting for assessments.

| Semester | Teaching Period | Exam Window |
|---|---|---|
| Odd Semester | July – October | November |
| Even Semester | December – March | April / May |

<Info>
Specific exam dates vary by programme and year. Always refer to the official timetable published on [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) for the exact schedule applicable to your course.
</Info>

## Finding the Examination Timetable

The examination timetable is published on the official exam portal well in advance of each exam window. You can access it by visiting [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) and navigating to the **Examination Time Table** section. Timetables are organised by programme and semester, so look for the one that matches your course.

<Tip>
Save or screenshot your programme's timetable as soon as it's published so you always have it handy — even without internet access during exam days.
</Tip>

## Staying Updated with Notifications

The Examination Wing publishes notifications, circulars, and orders related to exams throughout the year. These cover updates such as rescheduled exams, special examinations, and important instructions for candidates. Check the **Notifications** section on [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) regularly so you never miss a critical update.

## Downloading Your Hall Ticket

Your hall ticket is your official permission slip to enter the examination hall. You must download and carry a printed copy on every exam day — it will not be accepted on a phone screen. Hall tickets are available on both the exam portal and the students portal.

<Warning>
Always carry a **printed copy** of your hall ticket to the examination hall. Digital or on-screen copies are not accepted. Arrive early on your first exam day to avoid entry issues.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the Exam Portal">
    Go to [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) or [estudents.cusat.ac.in](https://estudents.cusat.ac.in) and sign in with your registered credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Hall Ticket Section">
    Once logged in, find the **Hall Ticket** section on the portal dashboard. If hall tickets are available for the current examination season, the download link will be active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download and Print Your Hall Ticket">
    Click the download link to save your hall ticket as a PDF. Print it on A4-size paper. Ensure all details — your name, register number, programme, and exam schedule — are clearly visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Timetable for Your Exam Dates">
    Cross-reference your hall ticket with the published **Examination Time Table** on [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) to confirm the dates, times, and venue for each of your papers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report to the Exam Hall">
    On exam day, arrive at least 15–20 minutes before the scheduled start time. Carry your printed hall ticket along with a valid government-issued photo ID. You will not be permitted entry without both.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Questions

<Accordion title="When will hall tickets be available for download?">
Hall tickets are typically made available a week or two before the examination window opens. Watch the **Notifications** section on [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) for the announcement, or check [cusat.ac.in/news](https://cusat.ac.in/news) for updates.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if my hall ticket has incorrect information?">
If you notice any discrepancy in your hall ticket — such as a wrong name, register number, or subject listing — contact the Examination Wing immediately at **0484-2862240** or **0484-2577109** before your exam date.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I download my hall ticket from the students portal?">
Yes. Both [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) and [estudents.cusat.ac.in](https://estudents.cusat.ac.in) provide hall ticket downloads. Use whichever portal you are already registered on.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I find previous exam timetables?">
The Examination Wing retains timetables from past semesters on the portal. Navigate to the **Examination Time Table** section on [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) and look for the archive of past schedules.
</Accordion>
