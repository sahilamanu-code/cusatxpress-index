The Examination Wing is the central body at CUSAT responsible for planning and executing all university-level examinations across its 137 academic programmes. From scheduling exam dates to publishing results and issuing official certificates, the Wing ensures the entire assessment process runs smoothly and transparently for every student enrolled at the university.

## The Semester Exam System

CUSAT follows a semester system, meaning the academic year is divided into two semesters — each with its own examination window. Exams are timed so they do not disrupt regular teaching hours, keeping classroom learning uninterrupted right through the teaching period.

| Semester | Teaching Period | Examination Window |
|---|---|---|
| Odd Semester | July – October | November |
| Even Semester | December – March | April / May |

At the end of each semester, the Examination Wing schedules and conducts all programme examinations, collects answer scripts, forwards them for valuation, tabulates the resulting marks, and then publishes official results.

## What the Examination Wing Does

The Wing manages the end-to-end lifecycle of every university examination:

- **Scheduling** – Publishes the examination timetable for all programmes without clashing with teaching schedules.
- **Conducting exams** – Oversees the smooth and fair conduct of all written examinations.
- **Valuation** – Forwards answer scripts to examiners and coordinates the valuation process.
- **Marks tabulation** – Compiles and tabulates marks received from examiners.
- **Results publication** – Publishes semester results on the official exam portal.
- **Certificates & mark lists** – Issues marklists, consolidated marksheets, degree certificates, migration certificates, and other academic documents.
- **Notifications & circulars** – Releases orders, notifications, and circulars on examination matters including malpractice cases.

## Key Portals

You can access exam-related services through two official portals:

- **[exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in)** – Hall ticket downloads, results, timetables, online certificate applications, notifications, and regulations.
- **[estudents.cusat.ac.in](https://estudents.cusat.ac.in)** – Student portal for admissions, personal details, hall tickets, and results.

<Note>
Both portals are maintained by CUSAT. For hall tickets and results, either portal works — use whichever you are already logged in to.
</Note>
