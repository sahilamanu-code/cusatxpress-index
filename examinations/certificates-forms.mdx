After completing your programme — or at any stage when you need official academic documentation — the CUSAT Examination Wing issues a range of certificates and documents that serve as proof of your qualifications. You can apply for most of these online through the exam portal, making the process straightforward regardless of where you are located.

## Documents Available

The Examination Wing issues the following certificates and academic documents:

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Marklist / Mark Certificate** | Subject-wise marks for a specific semester |
| **Consolidated Marksheet** | Cumulative marks across all semesters of your programme |
| **Degree Certificate** | Official certificate of degree award after programme completion |
| **Provisional Certificate** | Issued before the official degree certificate, useful for immediate employment or admissions |
| **Migration Certificate** | Required when transferring to another university |
| **Equivalence Certificate** | For academic purposes — confirms equivalence of your qualification |
| **Recognition Certificate** | For employment or higher education outside CUSAT |
| **Transcript** | Official record of your academic performance, often required for foreign universities |

<Info>
The **Equivalence Certificate** is issued for academic purposes (such as further study), while the **Recognition Certificate** is intended for employment or higher education admission purposes. Make sure you apply for the one that matches your requirement.
</Info>

## How to Apply Online

Most certificates can be applied for through the **Online Application for Certificates** section on the official exam portal. Here is how to complete the process:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Exam Portal">
    Go to [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in) and log in with your registered credentials. If you are a recent graduate, your login should still be active — contact the Wing if you have trouble accessing it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Online Application for Certificates">
    From the portal menu, select **Online Application for Certificates**. You will see a list of available documents you can apply for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Certificate You Need">
    Choose the specific certificate or document you require — for example, Consolidated Marksheet, Migration Certificate, or Transcript. Read any instructions displayed on the page before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the Application Form">
    Enter the required details, which may include your register number, programme, batch year, and purpose of the certificate. Double-check all entries before submitting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the Applicable Fee">
    Each certificate carries a prescribed fee. Complete the payment online as directed on the portal. Keep the payment receipt or transaction ID for your records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and Track Your Application">
    Submit the application. You will receive a confirmation on the portal. Track the status of your application through the same portal, and collect or receive your certificate once it is ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Offline / Physical Application Forms

If you prefer to apply in person, or if a particular document requires physical submission, application forms are available at the Examination Wing office on the CUSAT campus.

<Tip>
Carry copies of all relevant supporting documents — hall ticket, previous marklists, ID proof — when you visit the Examination Wing for physical applications. This avoids multiple trips.
</Tip>

## Common Questions

<Accordion title="How long does it take to receive a certificate after applying?">
Processing times vary depending on the type of certificate and the current workload of the Examination Wing. Check the portal for status updates, or contact the enquiry numbers if you have an urgent requirement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I get my certificates couriered to me?">
Check the online application form for courier or postal delivery options at the time of applying. Some documents may be available for dispatch; others may require in-person collection.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between a Degree Certificate and a Provisional Certificate?">
A **Provisional Certificate** is issued soon after you complete your programme, before the formal convocation. The official **Degree Certificate** is awarded at or after the convocation ceremony. Both are accepted for most official purposes, but some institutions may specifically require the final Degree Certificate.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I need a transcript for a foreign university application. What should I do?">
Apply for a **Transcript** through the Online Application for Certificates on [exam.cusat.ac.in](https://exam.cusat.ac.in). If the receiving institution requires the transcript to be sent directly, contact the Examination Wing at 0484-2862240 for guidance on the procedure.
</Accordion>
