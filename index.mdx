Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT) is a premier autonomous university established in 1971 and located at Thrikkakara, Kochi, Kerala. Ranked in the Times Higher Education and QS World University Rankings and re-accredited with **NAAC 'A+' Grade**, CUSAT offers more than 137 academic programmes across Engineering, Marine Sciences, Technology, Law, Management, and the Sciences — serving over 9,000 students across three campuses.

## Quick Start Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the CAT Schedule">
    CUSAT conducts its Common Admission Test (CAT) once a year. Visit the [CAT Schedule](/admissions/cat-schedule) page to find important dates — application window, exam date, and results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply Online">
    Register and submit your application at [admissions.cusat.ac.in](https://admissions.cusat.ac.in). See the [CAT Application Guide](/admissions/cat-application) for a step-by-step walkthrough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Your Allotment & Join">
    After the rank list is published, participate in centralised allotment to secure your seat. Review [Fees & Forms](/admissions/fee-structure) and complete your admission formalities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Student Resources">
    Once enrolled, log in to the [eStudents Portal](https://estudents.cusat.ac.in) to access your academic records, hall tickets, results, and campus services.
  </Step>
</Steps>
