Living on campus is one of the best decisions you can make as a CUSAT student. On-campus housing puts you close to your classrooms, laboratories, and the library, while surrounding you with a community of fellow learners from across India and beyond. CUSAT operates **13 hostels** across its campuses, accommodating approximately **1,300 students**, with separate, well-maintained facilities for male and female residents.

## Hostel Accommodation

<Info>
  All hostel admissions and fee payments are managed through the **online hostel portal** at **[hostels.cusat.ac.in](https://hostels.cusat.ac.in)**. You can apply for hostel accommodation, check availability, and make payments through this portal.
</Info>

<Note>
  Hostel seats are limited and are typically allocated on a first-come, first-served basis with priority given to students from outside Kerala and those from economically weaker sections. Apply early through **[hostels.cusat.ac.in](https://hostels.cusat.ac.in)** to secure your accommodation.
</Note>

## Dining — Campus Café

Good food is essential to good study. CUSAT's **Campus Café** provides affordable, nutritious meals to students, faculty, and staff across the Main Campus, ensuring that you always have access to a hot, satisfying meal without having to leave campus.

### Campus Café Details

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Opening Hours" icon="clock">
    The Campus Café is open **8 AM to 8 PM** on all working days, including **Saturdays**, giving you flexible meal and snack options throughout the day.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cuisine" icon="utensils">
    The Café serves a variety of **South Indian and North Indian dishes**, catering to the diverse tastes of CUSAT's national and international student community.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Affordable Meals" icon="indian-rupee-sign">
    Full meals are available at **nominal rates**, ensuring that every student can access wholesome food regardless of their financial situation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Daily Specials" icon="bowl-food">
    The menu rotates regularly to offer variety, with breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and dinner options available within the operating hours.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  In addition to the Campus Café, the Main Campus has several canteens located near individual departments and hostels. These informal eating spots are great places to grab a quick snack between classes and catch up with friends.
</Tip>

## Hostel Life Beyond the Room

Living in a CUSAT hostel is about much more than just a place to sleep. Hostels serve as the social heartbeat of campus — where late-night study groups form, friendships are forged across disciplines, and the informal learning that complements your classroom education truly happens. Hostel common rooms, reading rooms, and recreational areas provide spaces for both study and relaxation.

<Info>
  For queries, applications, and hostel-related support, visit the official hostel portal: **[hostels.cusat.ac.in](https://hostels.cusat.ac.in)**
</Info>
