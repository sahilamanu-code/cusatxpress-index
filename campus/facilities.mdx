A great university is more than its classrooms and laboratories — it is a complete ecosystem that supports your daily life, well-being, and long-term aspirations. CUSAT's Main Campus at Thrikkakara has been developed over decades into a self-sufficient community where you can study, live, bank, stay healthy, cycle, and connect with the world without ever leaving campus grounds.

## Employment Bureau

The **CUSAT Employment Bureau** serves as your link between academic life and the professional world. Established on **10 January 1973**, it is one of the oldest placement-support services at an Indian university.

<Info>
  The Employment Bureau maintains a database of student profiles, coordinates campus recruitment drives, and liaises with companies across IT, engineering, management, and research sectors to bring placement opportunities directly to you.
</Info>

## Eco-Friendly Transport — MyByk Bicycles

<Card title="MyByk Bicycle Sharing" icon="bicycle">
  CUSAT is connected to **Kochi Metro's MYBYK** bicycle sharing system. You can pick up and drop off bicycles at designated docking stations on campus, making short trips across the sprawling Main Campus faster, greener, and more enjoyable.

## Green Initiatives

CUSAT is deeply committed to environmental stewardship. Two flagship initiatives on the Main Campus demonstrate this commitment:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Miyawaki Mini Forest" icon="tree">
    A **Miyawaki Mini Forest** of approximately **1,600 native saplings** has been planted on campus using the Japanese Miyawaki method, which creates dense, biodiverse forest patches that grow up to 10 times faster than conventional planting. This initiative improves air quality, biodiversity, and the campus microclimate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rain Harvesting Pond" icon="droplet">
    A **rainwater harvesting pond**, commissioned in **2017**, collects and stores monsoon runoff for campus use. This facility reduces dependence on municipal water supply and serves as a model for sustainable water management in educational institutions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
