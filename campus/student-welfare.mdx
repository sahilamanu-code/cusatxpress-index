At CUSAT, your education extends well beyond the classroom. The university's robust student welfare ecosystem ensures that you have access to counselling, financial aid, health support, community service opportunities, cultural events, and sports — all within a campus environment that values your holistic development. From the moment you enrol, a network of dedicated offices, cells, and student-led organisations is ready to support you.

## Department of Youth Welfare

The **Department of Youth Welfare** is the central hub for student support at CUSAT, coordinating a wide range of services and activities designed to help you thrive academically, socially, and personally.

<Accordion title="Counselling and Personal Support">
  The Youth Welfare department provides individual and group **counselling services** to help you navigate the academic pressures, personal challenges, and transition experiences that are a normal part of university life. Trained counsellors are available to support your mental and emotional well-being throughout your time at CUSAT.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Training and Skill Development">
  Beyond academic training, the department organises workshops, seminars, and skill development programmes to help you build communication, leadership, and professional competencies that complement your degree.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="NSS and NCC">
  The department oversees CUSAT's **National Service Scheme (NSS)** and **National Cadet Corps (NCC)** units, offering you structured pathways for community service, leadership development, and national service. These are among the most valued extra-curricular credentials on your résumé.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Government-Sponsored Youth Projects">
  CUSAT participates in a range of **government-sponsored youth development projects**, giving you access to national-level competitions, exchange programmes, and project grants that extend your reach beyond the campus.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Financial Aid and Scholarships">
  The department coordinates **financial aid disbursements and scholarship applications**, ensuring that eligible students receive merit-based, need-based, and government-mandated scholarships without unnecessary delays.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Health Care">
  Working in coordination with the campus Health Centre, the Youth Welfare department facilitates access to **health care services** for students, including referrals to specialist care and support during medical emergencies.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Games, Sports, and Cultural Activities">
  From organising inter-departmental tournaments to facilitating your participation in national university sports events, the Youth Welfare department keeps campus life vibrant through **games, sports, and cultural activities** year-round.
</Accordion>

<Info>
  For more information about Youth Welfare activities, scholarships, and upcoming events, visit **[welfarecusat.in](https://welfarecusat.in)**.
</Info>

## National Service Scheme (NSS)

The **National Service Scheme (NSS)** at CUSAT embodies the spirit of selfless community service. Operating under the motto:

> *"Not Me But You"*

The NSS encourages you to look beyond personal ambitions and contribute to the community around you. NSS volunteers at CUSAT participate in literacy drives, environmental campaigns, rural development projects, health awareness programmes, and disaster relief activities. Completing your NSS programme also earns you a nationally recognised certificate that demonstrates your commitment to public service.

<Tip>
  Participation in NSS is not just about service — it builds qualities like empathy, teamwork, and civic responsibility that are valued by employers and graduate schools alike. Consider enrolling in the NSS programme early in your first year.
</Tip>

## Equal Opportunity Cell

CUSAT's **Equal Opportunity Cell** works to ensure that the university's reservation policy for **Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST)** students is implemented fairly and effectively. The cell:

- Monitors the admission, scholarship, and welfare entitlements of SC/ST students
- Provides a confidential grievance channel for students who face discrimination
- Coordinates with government agencies to ensure timely disbursement of reserved scholarships and financial benefits
- Promotes awareness of constitutional rights and entitlements among the student community

<Note>
  If you are an SC/ST student and need support regarding reservations, scholarships, or any form of discrimination, the Equal Opportunity Cell is your dedicated point of contact. All representations are treated with complete confidentiality.
</Note>

## Students Union

The **CUSAT Students Union** is your elected democratic voice within the university. The union represents the interests of the student community before university authorities, organises major cultural and academic events, and creates platforms for student leadership development.

<Info>
  Engage with your student government at **[studentsunion.cusat.ac.in](https://studentsunion.cusat.ac.in)**. Union elections are held annually, and every enrolled student is eligible to vote and stand for election.
</Info>

## Physical Education

The **Department of Physical Education (DPE)** at CUSAT manages all sports facilities, inter-university competitions, and physical fitness programmes. Whether you are a competitive athlete or someone who simply wants to stay active, the DPE offers:

## Mind Behaviour Studio

CUSAT's **Mind Behaviour Studio** is dedicated entirely to student well-being. Recognising that academic success and mental health are inseparable, the studio offers:

- Individual and group **counselling sessions** with trained psychologists
- **Workshops** on stress management, mindfulness, and resilience-building
- A safe, confidential space where you can discuss personal challenges without fear of judgement

<Tip>
  You do not need to be in crisis to visit the Mind Behaviour Studio. Many students find that regular check-ins with a counsellor help them maintain focus, manage exam stress, and build healthy habits for life.
</Tip>
