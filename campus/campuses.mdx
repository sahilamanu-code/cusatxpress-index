Whether you are studying engineering on the expansive Main Campus, researching ocean sciences at the Lakeside Campus, or pursuing engineering education amid the backwaters at Pulincunnu, every campus offers a uniquely immersive environment designed to support learning, research, and personal growth.

## Main Campus — Thrikkakara, Kalamassery

The **Main Campus** in Thrikkakara, Kalamassery is CUSAT's administrative and academic nerve centre. Spread across **180 acres** of green, well-maintained grounds, it is one of the largest and best-equipped university campuses in Kerala. You will find the bulk of CUSAT's academic, administrative, and social life concentrated here.

### What You Will Find

- **Administrative Offices** — The central administration, including the Vice Chancellor's office, Registrar, Finance Officer, and all major administrative units
- **Central Library** — One of the finest university libraries in India, housing a vast collection of books, journals, theses, and digital resources
- **Computer Centre** — Inaugurated by the Prime Minister of India in 1990, offering high-performance computing resources and internet infrastructure
- **Academic Departments and Laboratories** — The majority of CUSAT's science, technology, and humanities departments, equipped with modern research and teaching laboratories
- **Schools and Workshops** — Including the **School of Engineering**, with its fully equipped workshops and technical laboratories
- **Campus Café and Canteens** — Multiple dining options serving hot meals and refreshments throughout the day
- **Guest House** — On-campus accommodation for visiting faculty, researchers, and official guests
- **Hostels** — Multiple hostels for both male and female students (see the Hostels & Dining page for details)
- **Playgrounds and Sports Facilities** — Open grounds for cricket, football, athletics, and other sports

### Getting Here

<Note>
  The Main Campus is located **1.5 km off National Highway 47** in Thrikkakara, Kalamassery. It is well connected by KSRTC bus services, auto-rickshaws, and the Kochi Metro (Thrikkakara/Petta station is nearby). The campus is approximately 18 km from Cochin International Airport.
</Note>

## Lakeside Campus — Fine Arts Avenue, Pallimukku

The **Lakeside Campus** at Fine Arts Avenue, Pallimukku is situated approximately **12 km from the Main Campus**, right on the shores of Kochi's famous backwaters. This campus is CUSAT's dedicated home for marine and ocean sciences — and the setting itself, with the shimmering waters of Vembanad Lake as a backdrop, makes it one of the most evocative academic environments in the country.

### Schools and Departments Housed Here

- **School of Ocean Science and Technology**
- **School of Industrial Fisheries**
- Department of Atmospheric Sciences
- Department of Chemical Oceanography
- Department of Marine Biology, Microbiology, and Biochemistry
- Department of Marine Geology and Geophysics
- Department of Physical Oceanography

### The Marine Science Library

<Info>
  The Lakeside Campus is home to one of the **largest marine science libraries in Asia**, a treasure trove of specialised books, journals, research reports, and archival material on oceanography, marine biology, fisheries, and related fields. If you are pursuing research in the ocean sciences, this library is an unparalleled resource.
</Info>

### Research at the Water's Edge

The Lakeside Campus's proximity to Kochi's harbour, estuaries, and the Arabian Sea makes it an ideal base for fieldwork in oceanography, marine ecology, and coastal science. You have direct access to research vessels, sampling equipment, and a natural laboratory that few universities in the world can match.

## Pulincunnu Campus — Kuttanad, Alappuzha

The **Pulincunnu Campus** is located in Kuttanad, Alappuzha District, approximately **65 km from the Main Campus**. Kuttanad is famously known as the **"Venice of the East"** — a region of narrow waterways, paddy fields below sea level, and a way of life intimately bound to water. Studying here is an experience unlike any other in Indian higher education.

### Institutions at Pulincunnu

- **CUCEK** — Cochin University College of Engineering, Kuttanad, offering engineering programmes in a unique backwater setting
- **CUCCA** — Cochin University College of Commerce and Administration, providing management and commerce education to students from the Kuttanad region

### A Campus Surrounded by Water

<Tip>
  The Pulincunnu Campus is genuinely surrounded by water on multiple sides. Getting to campus often involves a scenic boat ride or journey across narrow bridges — an everyday reminder of the extraordinary natural environment that makes this campus unique. If you are looking for an immersive, unhurried academic setting away from city distractions, Pulincunnu offers an experience you will treasure for a lifetime.
</Tip>

## Choosing Your Campus

Each of CUSAT's three campuses offers a distinct academic focus and lifestyle. Your campus will largely be determined by the programme you enrol in, but all students share the common identity of being part of the CUSAT community — with access to central resources, inter-campus events, and the university's broader network of faculty and alumni.
